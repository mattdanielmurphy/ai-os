# Explainer reels: ideas

Matt's ideas for reels, recorded as he gave them. Nothing here is built until he 👍s it.

## 2026-10-24 Scroll-to-read presentation style

- **New style: a scrollable page for reading**, slightly more text-heavy, with live narration and word-by-word highlighting.
- **Highlighter blob behind each word.** A big scroll gesture makes the narrator skip ahead (small scrolls don't).
  - As the finger drags down, the blob is pulled downward, like an attraction that grows with the stretch.
  - Past the threshold, the blob flies to whatever is now at the vertical centre of the screen and stays centred while scrolling continues.
  - On finger lift, narration resumes from that point.
- **Layout as visual as possible**, even when it's still text.
- **Ambient music generator script**: background music from a random seed, no or truly minimal inference. (Real-musician rule doesn't block this: it's procedural, not AI-generated music.)
- **Small sound effects** for text appearing and transitions.
- **Auto read-aloud can be turned off**; if the device is muted, narration isn't generated automatically.

- **Scroll-to-read: middle-drag "third stage" (Matt, 2026-10-24).** Middle-button drag now moves the page like a finger. Maybe later: keep holding past a point and it keeps scrolling on its own (Windows-style autoscroll). Deferred to keep it simple.
