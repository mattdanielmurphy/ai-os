---

### 1. Why the Address "Loophole" Doesn’t Work
- **Canada vs. US:** In Canada, banks only verify your identity against your Canadian credit file (and a Canadian driver’s licence). 
- **The US Catch-22:** Under US federal law (the Patriot Act), US banks and credit card issuers require a US SSN/ITIN, US credit history, and verified US residency. Applying online to US banks with a friend's address will get auto-rejected or flagged for fraud.

---

### 2. The Recommended Setup (Do This from Canada)

#### Step 1: Open a Cross-Border US Bank Account & Credit Card (Primary Banking)
- **Providers:** **RBC Bank (U.S.)**, **TD Bank USA**, or **CIBC Bank USA**.
- **How it works:** Open it online or by phone while still in Canada using your **Canadian address, Canadian ID, and Canadian credit score**.
- **What you get:** 
  - A real US-domiciled checking account (US routing/account numbers for direct deposit & payroll).
  - **Zelle support** (the US equivalent of Interac e-Transfer for rent and splitting bills).
  - A **true US credit card** issued before you even board the plane.

#### Step 2: Use Wise as the FX / Transfer Bridge
- **Role:** Don't use Wise as your sole US bank (it lacks Zelle and doesn't build US credit), but **do use it to move your money**.
- **Why:** Canadian banks charge a 1.5%–2.5% hidden spread on currency conversions. Wise lets you convert Canadian savings into USD and deposit it directly into your new US bank account at true mid-market exchange rates.

#### Step 3: Fast-Track Premium Credit via Amex Global Transfer (Optional)
- If you have an existing Canadian Amex (held for 3+ months), you can open a **US American Express card** based on your Canadian credit history and Canadian passport—no US SSN needed initially.

---

### 3. What to Do Once in the US

1. **Update Address:** Once you sign a lease or move in, update your address from Canadian to your new US address directly inside your cross-border bank portal.
2. **Link the SSN:** When your US Social Security Number arrives in the mail (usually 2–3 weeks after entry), call your cross-border bank and Amex to attach the SSN. 
3. **Instant US Credit Score:** This immediately links all your existing account activity to the US credit bureaus (Experian/Equifax/TransUnion), giving you an established US credit score right out of the gate rather than starting from zero.
