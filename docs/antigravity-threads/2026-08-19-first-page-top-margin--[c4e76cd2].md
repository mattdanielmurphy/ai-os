Yes, exactly. RenderCV hardcodes an inclusive `+ 1` into its elapsed month formula inside its Python codebase (`rendercv/renderer/templater/date.py`):

```python

### RenderCV's internal formula:
timespan_in_days = (end_date_object - start_date_object).days
how_many_years = timespan_in_days // 365
how_many_months = (timespan_in_days % 365) // 30 + 1  # <-- Forcefully adds +1 month
```

Because RenderCV does this internally for all date ranges, **every single job on the resume is automatically having 1 extra month added to its duration** (e.g., Jan 1993 to August 2026 computes to 7 full elapsed months, but RenderCV turns it into `33 years 8 months`).

### How to Fix This for All Dates
We have two clean ways to fix this across the entire resume:

1. **Option A (Template Duration Calculator)**: Override the date-span formatting in our local `classic/entries/ExperienceEntry.j2.typ` template so that Typst calculates exact elapsed completed calendar months (or formats the exact completed tenure) instead of relying on RenderCV's `+ 1` formula.
2. **Option B (Disable Automatic Time Spans & Explicit Ranges)**: Disable `show_time_spans_in: []` in `darryl_murphy_cv.yaml` so only the clean dates (`Jan 1993 – present`, `Oct 2022 – May 2026`, etc.) appear without duration sub-labels (which is standard for executive CVs).
