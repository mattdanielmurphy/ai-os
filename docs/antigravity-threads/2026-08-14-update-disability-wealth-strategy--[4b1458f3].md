#### 3. High-Spec Machine & RAM Justification Strategy
* **Local Processing Justification**: Frame the laptop requirement around **local speech-to-text inference latency**. * **The "Co-Pay / Top-Up" Mechanism**: If the approved grant amount is $3,000 CAD and the desired machine is $4,200 CAD, you simply pay the difference using flat student grants (such as the $2,800 CSGD or $3,000 GFD). Submitting the complete Apple invoice clears the grant audit.
* **Apple Education & Refurbished Stacking**: Leverage the Apple Higher Education Store (~10% discount) and Apple Certified Refurbished inventory to maximize hardware specs per dollar.
* **Complete Anonymity of Existing Devices**: Alberta Student Aid and U of A ARDS have zero record of what laptops or personal gear you already own. They only verify whether you have received a previous computer grant through Schedule 4 within the last 3–4 years.
