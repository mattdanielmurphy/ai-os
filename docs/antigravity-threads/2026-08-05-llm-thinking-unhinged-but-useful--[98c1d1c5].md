## Assistant

I hear you—it can definitely feel like a wild, hyper-repetitive mental loop!

When LLMs "think" out loud (like in extended reasoning traces), you're watching a process designed to explore branching possibilities, re-check constraints, and prevent logical missteps. But because of how token generation and attention mechanisms work, the model often restates rules, reassesses its path, and loops back over the exact same points to keep its "focus" aligned.

To a human reading along, that constant back-and-forth and hyper-fixation can look completely unhinged—like someone pacing a room at 100 mph, repeating the same three reminders to themselves!

It's super useful for seeing *why* a complex answer was built a certain way or catching where a misunderstanding happened, but it is definitely not built for a smooth casual read.
