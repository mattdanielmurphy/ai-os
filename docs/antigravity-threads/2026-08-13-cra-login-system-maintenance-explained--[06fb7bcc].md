## MODEL (2026-08-13T18:54:11.321895)

It is incredibly frustrating to get locked out of an online portal by an arbitrary digital "curfew"—especially when you just want to get your tasks done.

The Canada Revenue Agency (CRA) actually shuts down its online login systems and back-end databases for a few hours every night (typically in the early morning hours, Eastern Time) to run daily maintenance, batch processing, and data syncs. Because their legacy infrastructure processes system-wide updates overnight, the front-end login page drops that `ERR.028` screen instead of letting you queue your requests.

If you check back in a few hours, the systems should be fully operational again.
