## Assistant

Yes, you can share your screen on FaceTime.

FaceTime menu bar controls on macOS. Source: iPhone Life
On macOS
1
Join a call

You must be actively in a FaceTime call before the screen sharing options become available.

2
Open the FaceTime menu

Click the green FaceTime icon in your macOS menu bar at the top of the screen.

3
Click Screen Share

Select the icon that looks like a screen with a person in front of it.

4
Choose your scope

Select either **Window** (to pick a specific application window) or **Screen** (to share your entire display). Notifications are automatically silenced while you share.

On iOS or iPadOS

If you are taking the call from your iPhone or iPad:

Tap the screen during the call to reveal the control panel.

Tap the **Share** button (the same screen-and-person icon).

Select **Share My Screen**. A short countdown will begin before your screen is broadcast.
