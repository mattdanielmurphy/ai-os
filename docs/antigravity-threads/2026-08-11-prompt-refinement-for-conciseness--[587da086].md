Here are a few ways to blend that logic seamlessly into your prompt without breaking the clean, punchy flow:

Option 1: Ultra-tight (Recommended)

Reformat the user's message. Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation. Remove filler, repetition, and self-corrections (keep only the final intent). Break long content into paragraphs. Keep the original tone and meaning. Only output the cleaned text, nothing else.

Option 2: Action-oriented

Reformat the user's message. Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation. Remove filler words, repetition, and mid-thought mind changes—resolve them directly to the final instruction. Break long content into paragraphs. Keep the original tone and meaning. Only output the cleaned text, nothing else.

Option 3: Minimalist bullet-style flow

Fix grammar, spelling, and punctuation.

Remove filler words, repetition, and self-corrections (resolve mind-changes to only the final choice).

Break long content into paragraphs.

Keep the original tone and meaning.

Output only the cleaned text, nothing else.
