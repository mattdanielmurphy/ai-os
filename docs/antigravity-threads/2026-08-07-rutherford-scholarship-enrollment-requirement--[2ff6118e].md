The current Alberta Student Aid operational policy provides definitive criteria regarding the enrollment requirements for the Alexander Rutherford Scholarship.

Source & Context Audit

Official documentation from Alberta Student Aid dictates that the scholarship is only processed and disbursed upon verified registration in a qualifying post-secondary program. Offers of admission do not satisfy this requirement.

Explicit Assumptions & Boundaries

This assumes you plan to take at least 60% of a full course load (full-time status), as part-time enrollment does not qualify.  
Alberta Student Aid - Government of Alberta

This applies to undergraduate programs at designated institutions, which includes both the University of Alberta and MacEwan University.  
Alberta Student Aid - Government of Alberta

**Yes, you must be officially enrolled.** You cannot apply while you are still deciding between your university options.  
Canada GSL - Grants and Student Loans

An offer of admission is not enough to secure the scholarship for a few key reasons:

**Institution Matching:** The application requires you to specify the exact post-secondary institution you will be attending. You cannot leave this blank or list multiple options.  
Canada GSL - Grants and Student Loans

**Direct Verification:** Once you apply, Alberta Student Aid contacts your chosen university directly to confirm your registration.  
Canada GSL - Grants and Student Loans

**Course Load Proof:** The university must verify that you are actively registered in classes that constitute at least a 60% course load.  
Alberta Student Aid - Government of Alberta

Because there is no fixed application deadline for the Alexander Rutherford Scholarship, there is no penalty for waiting. You simply need to make your final choice, accept the offer, enroll in your courses, and ensure your official high school transcripts are finalized before submitting your application.
