7-Day High-Protein GLP-1 Meal Plan (Mounjaro-Friendly)

*Built around: delayed gastric emptying, acid reflux protection, and muscle preservation.*

Clinical Framework Behind This Plan

**Protein target:** Clinical advisories for people on GLP-1/GIP medications (tirzepatide/Mounjaro) recommend **1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight per day**, distributed across at least 3–4 eating occasions, with a **minimum of 20–30 g protein per meal** to trigger muscle protein synthesis and offset the ~25–39% of weight loss that can come from lean mass on these drugs. This plan is structured to hit protein density at *every* meal and snack, not just dinner, since appetite suppression makes it easy to under-eat protein overall.

**Reflux/LES protection:** Dietitian guidance for GLP-1 users emphasizes avoiding high-fat, fried, or heavy dairy meals within 2–3 hours of bedtime, keeping portions to 70–80% fullness (never "Thanksgiving full"), and pushing spicy/acidic foods (tomato sauce, hot sauce) earlier in the day since fat and large volumes slow gastric emptying further and can trigger reflux when lying down.

**Delayed gastric emptying:** Small, frequent meals (4–6x/day) eaten slowly, with low-to-moderate fat, prevent the nausea/bloating cycle. Liquids are capped at ~250 mL per sitting so shakes don't compound fullness on top of solid food.

**Anti-fasting structure:** A fixed 5-slot daily rhythm (Breakfast → Snack → Lunch → Snack → Dinner, +/- evening top-up) prevents the "too nauseated to eat" spiral that happens when meals get skipped and hunger cues get confused with GLP-1-induced nausea.

Daily Timing Template
Window	Time (approx)	Purpose
Breakfast	7:30–8:30 AM	Small, protein-forward, sets the day's rhythm
AM Snack	10:30–11:00 AM	Prevents pre-lunch crash/nausea
Lunch	12:30–1:30 PM	Main protein + can include spicy/acidic foods here
PM Snack	3:30–4:00 PM	Bridges to dinner, keeps protein steady
Dinner	6:00–6:30 PM	Lower fat, bland-leaning, earlier is better
Optional Evening Top-Up	8:00 PM (only if needed)	Small, bland, no fat/spice/carbonation — e.g. a few spoons of cottage cheese or a half protein bar

Rule of thumb: **stop at 70–80% full**, eat slowly, stay upright 2–3 hours post-meal, and don't lie down right after eating.

**Lunch (12:30 PM):** Simple chicken tacos (2 small, soft tortillas, shredded chicken, mild-to-medium salsa — spice goes here since it's midday). (~28g protein)

**Evening (optional, 8 PM):** 1/3 cup cottage cheese, plain. (~7g protein)

**Breakfast:** Small bowl of Cheerios with 2% milk + a few strawberries. (~9g protein)

**AM Snack:** 1/2 scoop LeanFit vanilla whey mixed into 200 mL 2% milk (stay under 250 mL). (~15g protein)

**Lunch:** Eggs on toast (2 eggs, scrambled) + raw bell pepper strips on the side. (~16g protein)

**PM Snack:** Pure Protein bar (Birthday Cake), eaten slowly in two sittings if needed. (~20g protein)

**Dinner:** Spätzle/egg noodles mixed with cottage cheese (small portion, this is a liked combo and naturally moderate-fat). (~20g protein)

**Evening (optional):** A few raspberries.

Day 3 (Pizza Day — placed strategically)

**Breakfast:** Scrambled eggs (2) on toast, no butter. (~14g protein)

**AM Snack:** Berry/yogurt parfait (small yogurt + blueberries/raspberries). (~8g protein)

**Lunch (12:30 PM — pizza slot):** 1–2 small slices of pizza, blotted of excess oil, eaten slowly. This is the earliest safe slot since pizza is high-fat and acidic (tomato sauce) — giving 5–6 hours before bed to digest. (~18g protein)

**PM Snack:** Cottage cheese (small) + a few blueberries. (~14g protein)

**Dinner (lighter, since lunch was heavier):** Corn on the cob + a boiled egg or two. (~14g protein)

**Evening (optional):** Skip if still full from lunch, or a few sips of milk only.

Day 4

**Lunch:** Chicken strips (2–3, baked not fried) + a few bites of corn on the cob. (~25g protein)

**PM Snack:** Fit Crunch bar (half now, half later if too full). (~15g protein)

**Dinner:** Chicken noodle soup, small bowl. (~15g protein)

**Evening (optional):** Small yogurt cup.

Day 5 (Spicy day, daytime only)

**Breakfast:** Boiled egg + toast, plain. (~10g protein)

**AM Snack:** Strawberries + a small handful of cottage cheese. (~7g protein)

**Lunch (spicy slot):** Chicken tacos with a spicier salsa or hot sauce — midday only. (~28g protein)

**PM Snack:** Pure Protein bar. (~20g protein)

**Dinner (bland, low-fat):** Egg noodles plain or with a little cottage cheese, no sauce. (~16g protein)

**Evening (optional):** A few raspberries, no dairy if reflux-prone that night.

Day 6

**Breakfast:** Cheerios + 2% milk + banana slices. (~9g protein)

**AM Snack:** Half scoop LeanFit whey in 200 mL milk. (~15g protein)

**Lunch:** Eggs on toast + raw bell peppers. (~16g protein)

**PM Snack:** Cottage cheese + strawberries. (~14g protein)

High-Protein, Minimal-Effort Snack/Meal Ideas

No-cook or near-zero-cook, built from your liked list:

**Cottage cheese + strawberries** — just scoop and top. (~14g protein per 1/2 cup)

**Cottage cheese + egg noodles/spätzle (leftover, cold or reheated)** — your favorite combo, ready in the time it takes to reheat.

**Berry/yogurt parfait** — yogurt + blueberries/raspberries, no assembly beyond opening containers.

**Peanut butter + cucumber slices** — protein/fat pairing that's gentle and filling in small amounts.

**Half a Fit Crunch bar or Pure Protein bar** — pre-portioned, zero prep; splitting bars helps manage volume.

**Boiled eggs (batch-cook 4–6 at once)** — grab-and-go protein, no daily cooking needed.

**LeanFit whey, half scoop in 200 mL milk** — stays under your 250 mL liquid cap; shake in a bottle, no blender needed.

**Eggs on toast** — 5-minute scramble, always in rotation.

**Cheerios + 2% milk + banana** — a "safe" low-effort meal for low-appetite mornings.

Timing Guidelines for Comfort/Trigger Foods

**Pizza:** Restrict to lunch or early afternoon (before ~2 PM). It's high-fat and often eaten with acidic sauce — both slow gastric emptying and relax the LES, so it needs the longest possible digestion window before lying down. Blot excess oil, keep it to 1–2 slices, and eat slowly.

**Tacos/spicy food:** Midday only (lunch window). Capsaicin and acidic salsas are reflux triggers; giving 5+ hours before bed reduces nighttime symptom risk.

**Tomato-based sauces in general:** Treat like pizza — daytime only, moderate portion.

**Protein shakes:** Cap at 250 mL per sitting; split a full scoop into two half-scoop servings across the day rather than one large shake, since large liquid volumes can worsen fullness/nausea on GLP-1s.

**Dairy-heavy dishes at night:** Keep dinner and evening top-ups light on dairy fat — cottage cheese in small amounts is fine, but avoid richer dairy (creamy sauces, large yogurt portions) close to bedtime.

**General rule:** Nothing high-fat, fried, spicy, or acidic within 3 hours of bedtime. Stay upright after eating, and don't recline for at least 2–3 hours post-meal.

## Perplexity — 12:55 AM

7-Day High-Protein GLP-1 Meal Plan (Mounjaro-Friendly)

*Built around: delayed gastric emptying, acid reflux protection, and muscle preservation.*

Clinical Framework Behind This Plan

**Protein target:** Clinical advisories for people on GLP-1/GIP medications (tirzepatide/Mounjaro) recommend **1.2–1.6 g of protein per kg of body weight per day**, distributed across at least 3–4 eating occasions, with a **minimum of 20–30 g protein per meal** to trigger muscle protein synthesis and offset the ~25–39% of weight loss that can come from lean mass on these drugs. This plan is structured to hit protein density at *every* meal and snack, not just dinner, since appetite suppression makes it easy to under-eat protein overall.

**Reflux/LES protection:** Dietitian guidance for GLP-1 users emphasizes avoiding high-fat, fried, or heavy dairy meals within 2–3 hours of bedtime, keeping portions to 70–80% fullness (never "Thanksgiving full"), and pushing spicy/acidic foods (tomato sauce, hot sauce) earlier in the day since fat and large volumes slow gastric emptying further and can trigger reflux when lying down.

**Delayed gastric emptying:** Small, frequent meals (4–6x/day) eaten slowly, with low-to-moderate fat, prevent the nausea/bloating cycle. Liquids are capped at ~250 mL per sitting so shakes don't compound fullness on top of solid food.

**Anti-fasting structure:** A fixed 5-slot daily rhythm (Breakfast → Snack → Lunch → Snack → Dinner, +/- evening top-up) prevents the "too nauseated to eat" spiral that happens when meals get skipped and hunger cues get confused with GLP-1-induced nausea.
