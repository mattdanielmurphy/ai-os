## Assistant

Doing it legitimately is the right move. Under the Alberta *Residential Tenancies Act* (RTA), sneaking an unauthorized pet into a non-pet-friendly building can trigger a 14-day eviction notice for breach of contract, along with heavy cleaning or carpet replacement fees upon move-out if allergic tenants complain.

In Edmonton, finding a **cat-friendly building that prohibits dogs** is actually very common. Many landlords in older low-rise and walk-up buildings explicitly set a "Cats Only / No Dogs" policy specifically to avoid noise and barking issues in woodframe construction.

**Garneau & Queen Alexandra / Strathcona (Near U of A)**

**Vibe:** Highly walkable student/young professional areas within a 5–15 minute walk to campus or Whyte Ave.

**Pricing:** Studios range from $900 to $1,200/mo; 1-bedroom apartments range from $1,150 to $1,450/mo.

**Dog Factor:** The older 3-story brick walk-ups lining 83rd–86th Avenues frequently enforce **"Cats Only"** rules because woodframe floors amplify dog barking.

**Vibe:** Located just west of Downtown, extremely convenient for MacEwan (10–15 min walk) and direct to U of A via the Capital Line LRT (Grandin/Government Centre Station) or bus routes on Jasper Ave.

**Pricing:** 1-bedroom units in mid-rise and low-rise complexes routinely go for $1,150 to $1,380/mo.

**Dog Factor:** High concentration of cat-friendly low-rises along 110th to 124th Streets.

**Downtown & Grandin / Railtown**

**Vibe:** Right on the LRT line, bridging MacEwan and U of A within 5–10 minutes.

**Pricing:** Studios and smaller 1-beds from $1,100 to $1,400/mo (e.g., Square 104, Axis Tower, or Mayfair Village South).

How to Filter for "Cats Only" Buildings

**Use RentFaster.ca:** RentFaster is the primary rental platform in Alberta. Set your location filter to *Edmonton (Central/University)*, select **Cats Allowed**, and **uncheck Dogs Allowed**. This isolates properties that specifically exclude dogs.

**Target Low-Rise Walk-Ups over High-Rise Condos:** Concrete high-rises tend to allow dogs because sound transfer is lower. Low-rise woodframe buildings almost always prefer cats only.

**Watch for Monthly Pet Fees:** Most Alberta rental companies charge a non-refundable pet fee of **$25–$50/month per cat** (or a flat initial fee). Factor this into your $1,500 total budget.

3. Edmonton Walkability & The Free Street Parking Hack

If walkability and free parking are top priorities, focus on two specific pockets:

**Garneau / Old Strathcona (Walk Score: ~85–92):** You can walk to U of A in 5–10 minutes, take the LRT to MacEwan in 12 minutes, and walk to grocery stores (No Frills, Save-On) and Whyte Ave.

**Wîhkwêntôwin / Oliver (Walk Score: ~88–95):** 10-minute walk to MacEwan, 10-minute direct LRT ride to U of A from Grandin Station, and extremely high grocery density (Safeway, H-Mart, Loblaws CityCentre).

The Street Parking Hack:

Instead of paying **$75–$150/month** for a building's underground or surface parking stall, target low-rises located inside the **City of Edmonton Residential Parking Program (RPP) zones** (prominent in Garneau and Wîhkwêntôwin).

Once you sign a lease, you register your vehicle with the city using your lease agreement to get an annual resident permit. This allows you to park on restricted residential streets right outside your building while keeping commuters from taking your spot.
