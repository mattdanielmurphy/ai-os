Students attending a public post-secondary institution in Alberta must meet directly with an Accessibility Advisor at their school to have their Schedule 4 reviewed. The educational institution section of your Schedule 4 must be completed by that Accessibility Advisor or the Registrar's Office.

So, once you accept your offer (whether U of A or MacEwan), you need to meet specifically with their Accessibility/Disability Services office. You tell them you are a Schedule 4 student taking a 40% course load, and they complete the required section of the form to verify your status with the government.

3. How to Check Your Status

Since you submitted it months ago, it should be in the government's system, but you can verify where it currently sits:

**Online Portal:** Log in to your secure Alberta Student Aid account. From there, you can see your specific application status, check your payment schedule, and look at your inbox to see if they requested any missing documentation.

**By Phone:** You can call the Alberta Student Aid Service Centre directly at 1-855-606-2096. If you choose Option 1, you can ask an agent specifically about your application status.

**The Wait Time Reality:** Alberta Student Aid applications that require a manual review can take up to 45 days to process, and wait times often exceed that during the peak August period.

Checking your portal or giving them a quick call tomorrow will confirm if the paperwork is just sitting in their backlog, or if it is fully approved and simply waiting for you to get that final signature from your eventual school.
