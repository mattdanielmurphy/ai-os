For the **Fall 2026** semester, both MacEwan University and the University of Alberta (U of A) follow their standard undergraduate academic schedules for Computer Science / Computing Science:

---

### **1. MacEwan University** (Faculty of Arts and Science / Computer Science)
* **New Student Orientation:** Tuesday, September 1, 2026
* **First Day of Classes:** **Wednesday, September 2, 2026**
* *Note:* You can view your specific section schedule and room assignments on **myStudentSystem** via the MacEwan portal.

---

### **2. University of Alberta** (Faculty of Science / Computing Science)
* **Week of Welcome / Orientation:** Late August – Early September 2026
* **First Day of Classes:** **Tuesday, September 1, 2026**
* *Note:* You can confirm specific lecture/lab start dates for your registered courses on **[Bear Tracks](https://www.beartracks.ualberta.ca/)**.

---

*(For reference, Computing Science undergraduate classes at both institutions follow the regular main academic schedule rather than specialized faculty tracks like Law or Medicine.)*
