## Assistant

No, you can't.

In the UK, Uber operates strictly as a **Private Hire Vehicle (PHV)** service rather than a traditional street-hail taxi (a Hackney Carriage, like a London black cab).

By law, any car driving for a ride-share platform like Uber, Bolt, or a local minicab firm must be licensed by the local authority (council) where the driver operates.

To legally pick up passengers as an Uber driver in the UK:

The **driver** needs a private hire driver's licence.

The **vehicle** needs a private hire vehicle licence—which comes with those council-issued external licence plates/stickers that must be displayed on the car.

The **operator** (Uber itself) must hold a private hire operator licence in that jurisdiction.

Driving for Uber without displaying valid local council PHV plates is illegal and can lead to immediate prosecution, heavy fines, and loss of your driving licence.
