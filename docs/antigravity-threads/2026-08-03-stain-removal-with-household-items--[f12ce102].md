## Assistant

You can effectively treat the stain using either **dish soap** or **liquid laundry detergent**.

Recommended Method: Dish Soap or Liquid Laundry Detergent

**Flush with cold water:** Run cold tap water through the back of the stain to push as much of the substance out of the fabric fibers as possible. Avoid hot water, as it can set protein or tannin-based stains.

**Apply detergent directly:** Drizzle a small drop of dish soap (or a few drops of liquid laundry detergent) directly onto the spot.

**Work it in:** Gently rub the fabric together or use your fingertips to massage the detergent into the stain. Let it sit for 10 to 15 minutes.

**Rinse and check:** Rinse the area thoroughly with cold water.

**Wash as usual:** Wash the blanket in your regular laundry cycle according to its care label instructions.

**Important:** Inspect the blanket before putting it in the dryer. If any trace of the stain remains after washing, high heat will set it permanently. Repeat the spot treatment if necessary before drying.
