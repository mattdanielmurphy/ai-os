### Standalone vs. Combined Application PDFs

The standard and most effective practice across both ATS application portals and executive recruitment is to generate **both formats**:

1. **Standalone Cover Letter PDFs (`cover_letters/pdf/<Role_Title>_Cover_Letter.pdf`)**:
   - **Use Case**: ATS portals (Workday, Taleo, SuccessFactors, LinkedIn, Indeed) that have separate, mandatory upload slots for **"Resume"** and **"Cover Letter"**.
   - **Format**: Exactly 1 page, matching the typography (`Source Sans 3` / System UI), Executive Slate/Cobalt color palette (`#0f172a` / `#1d4ed8`), header banner, and credential badges of the resume.

2. **Combined Dossier / Application PDFs (`applications/<Role_Title>_Application_Package.pdf`)**:
   - **Use Case**: Direct email outreach to executive search consultants, hiring managers, and agency recruiters (e.g., Brunel, Airswift, TRS Staffing, Spencer Ogden).
   - **Format**: Page 1 is the tailored cover letter; Pages 2–3 are the tailored executive master resume, compiled into a single document so reviewers don't have to open multiple attachments.
