## Assistant

While Yousician locks most of its song library and unlimited practice time behind a subscription, several **free, web-based and Mac-compatible alternatives** offer massive song catalogs and interactive play-along features:

🎸 For Guitar, Bass & Drums
1. **Songsterr** *(Web)*

**Best for:** Scrolling real-time tabs and massive song availability.  
Reddit

**Song Catalog:** Over 800,000+ interactive tabs.

**Key Features:** Real-time scrolling playback (similar to Yousician’s sidescrolling view) with realistic synth audio, track isolation (mute/solo guitar, bass, or drums), tempo control, and loop sections.  
Reddit

**Cost:** 100% free web version (no account strictly required to play along).

2. **JustinGuitar.com** *(Web)*

**Best for:** Structured lessons + song tutorials.

**Song Catalog:** Hundreds of popularity-sorted song lessons filtered by difficulty and chord count.

**Key Features:** Full step-by-step video lessons, interactive chord/metronome tools, and practice routines.

**Cost:** 100% free on the web (*Note: Use the web version, as his mobile app uses a paid tier*).  
Reddit

3. **Soundslice** *(Web)*

**Best for:** Synced sheet music/tabs with real YouTube audio/video.

**Key Features:** Interactive player that syncs user-created tabs with original performance videos. Supports tempo slowdown, looping, and pitch modification.  
Reddit

**Cost:** Free web community library.  
Reddit

🎹 For Piano & Sheet Music
4. **Pianu** *(Web)*

**Best for:** Interactive web-based piano song play-along.

**Key Features:** Connects directly to any USB/MIDI keyboard in Safari or Chrome. Displays interactive falling-note sheet music and detects your keystrokes in real time.

**Cost:** Free browser tier with a solid catalog of popular and classical tracks.

5. **MuseScore** *(Web & Mac Native App)*

**Best for:** Real sheet music and arrangement playback.

**Song Catalog:** Over 1.5 million community-uploaded arrangements across every genre.

**Key Features:** The free open-source macOS app plays back any score note-by-note, supports MIDI input, lets you mute individual instruments, and allows you to adjust playback speed.  
Philly Music Lessons

**Cost:** 100% free open-source Mac app; free score viewing/listening on the web.

🥁 Multi-Instrument / Backing Tracks
6. **Jamzone** *(macOS App & Web)*

**Best for:** Jamming along with multitrack audio.  
www.jamzone.com

**Key Features:** Real-time synced chord diagrams, lyrics, and individual track volume sliders (mute the lead instrument so you can play it yourself).  
www.jamzone.com

**Cost:** Free tier available with a selection of full multitrack songs.

Quick Recommendation

**If you want Yousician's scrolling visualizer for Guitar/Bass:** Use **Songsterr**.  
Reddit

**If you want interactive MIDI feedback for Piano:** Use **Pianu** in your browser or **MuseScore** on macOS.  
Philly Music Lessons

**If you want step-by-step song lessons:** Use **JustinGuitar**.
