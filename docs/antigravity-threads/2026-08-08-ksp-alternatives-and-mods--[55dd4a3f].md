## Assistant

With the cancellation of *Kerbal Space Program 2* development and the closure of Intercept Games in 2024, the community has turned to a few key titles and projects to fill the void:

1. Top Games & Simulators
**Juno: New Origins** *(Formerly SimpleRockets 2)*

**Best for:** Direct 3D spaceflight simulation and procedural design.

**Overview:** Features fully procedural parts (fuel tanks, engines, wings), customizable celestial systems, realistic orbital mechanics, and an integrated visual scripting language (Vizzy) to automate launches and landings. It is currently the most complete 3D alternative to KSP.

**KitHack Model Club**

**Best for:** Physics-driven vehicle crafting from the original creator of KSP.

**Overview:** Created by Felipe Falanghe ("Harvester"), the original designer of Kerbal Space Program. While focused primarily on remote-controlled scale models (airplanes, boats, cars, and rockets), it uses a similar physics-first building system and passion for sandbox engineering.

**Spaceflight Simulator (SFS)**

**Best for:** Quick, accessible 2D rocket building and interplanetary travel.

**Overview:** A 2D space simulator with realistic orbital physics, staging, landing, and docking. Easy to pick up, with a large modding community on PC and mobile.

**Flyout**

**Best for:** Advanced aeronautics and aircraft design.

**Overview:** A physics-focused modeling simulator for aircraft design. It uses custom in-game modeling tools, freeform wing/fuselage shaping, and detailed engine physics. While tailored to flight rather than spaceflight, it appeals strongly to the engineering/plane-building side of the KSP audience.

2. Heavily Modded KSP 1 (The Reigning Standard)

For many players, heavily modded **Kerbal Space Program 1** remains the definitive space simulation experience. Popular overhaul paths include:

**Realism Overhaul (RO) + RP-1:** Converts KSP into a hardcore real-world space agency simulator using real fuels, realistic engine ignitions, avionics limits, and real-scale planets.

**Visual Overhauls:** Combines mods like *Parallax 2* (ground textures and flora), *Scatterer* (atmospheric scattering), and Blackrack's *Volumetric Clouds* to give KSP 1 visuals that rival modern releases.
