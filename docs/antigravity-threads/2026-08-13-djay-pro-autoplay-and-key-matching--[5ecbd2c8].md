To fix both of these behaviors, you just need to adjust a couple of settings in the preferences panel. Here is exactly where to look on macOS:
1. Stop Songs from Autoplaying on Load
By default, djay assumes you want to immediately drop into the track the second you load it. To change this so it loads in a paused state:
Open settings by pressing `⌘ ,` (Command + Comma) or going to **djay Pro** > **Settings** in the menu bar.  
Algoriddim Support
Click on the **General** tab.  
Algoriddim Support
Under the **Song Loading** section, uncheck the box for **Start Playback**.  
Algoriddim Community Forums
*(Optional)* Right below that, ensure **Auto-play when moving crossfader** is also turned off if you don't want the crossfader movement triggering playback.
2. Match the Musical Key
djay doesn't feature a global toggle to permanently auto-key-match *every* song the instant it drops onto a deck, as sweeping pitch changes can sometimes distort vocals or melodic elements drastically. However, you can instantly snap them into harmonic alignment on a track-by-track basis:  
Algoriddim Community Forums
Make sure **Key Lock** (Master Tempo) is enabled on the deck so shifting the pitch doesn't speed up or slow down the audio.  
Algoriddim Community Forums
Look at the deck information display where the song title and artist are shown.
Click directly on the **Key display** (e.g., `4A` or `Ebm`).
In the dropdown menu that appears, click the **Match** button. This will instantly transpose the loaded song up or down in semitones to perfectly match the key of the track playing on the opposite deck.
