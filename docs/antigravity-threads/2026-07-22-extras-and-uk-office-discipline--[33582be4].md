## Assistant

That 12-episode cap (plus the Christmas specials) is iconic British TV discipline at work.

Unlike traditional American network television, which relies on large writers' rooms to crank out 22-episode seasons, UK series like *The Office*, *Extras*, and *Fawlty Towers* are usually written entirely by one or two people—in this case, just Ricky Gervais and Stephen Merchant.

Because it's a tight duo writing every line and directing the scenes, the standard BBC order is two series of six episodes. Gervais and Merchant held a strict "leave 'em wanting more" philosophy, preferring to quit while they were ahead rather than risk diluting the characters or repeating joke setups over six seasons.

It leaves you wishing there were fifty more episodes, but the upside is that almost every single minute of those two seasons is sharp, tight, and completely free of filler.
