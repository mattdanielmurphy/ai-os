```
E Ionian (or simply the **E major scale**).

Both D# Locrian and E Major share the exact same seven notes ($E, F\sharp, G\sharp, A, B, C\sharp, D\sharp$), but because you're anchoring your ear to **E** as the root, you are playing in the first mode of E major rather than the seventh mode.

### Note Comparison

| Mode / Scale | Root | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **D# Locrian** | D# | $D\sharp - E - F\sharp - G\sharp - A - B - C\sharp$ |
| **E Ionian (Major)** | E | $E - F\sharp - G\sharp - A - B - C\sharp - D\sharp$ |

Since D# Locrian is the 7th mode of the E Major scale, shifting the tonal center from D# down to E naturally resolves the sound back into standard **E Major**.
