# Disable Facebook Group Notifications

## User

how to disable this kind of notification in facebook?

I don't see the option in notification settings

---

## Assistant

That specific push notification is a **Group Activity / Post Update** alert from a Facebook group you belong to ("Fit Mission Makeup with J..."). Facebook often labels these as general engagement or group notifications rather than standard page updates.

Here is how to stop these from appearing:

Option 1: Turn off notifications for this specific Group (Recommended)

If you want to stay in the group but stop receiving push alerts whenever someone posts:

Open the **Facebook app** and navigate to the group (**Fit Mission Makeup with J...**).

Tap the **Joined** button (or the three dots **•••** in the top right corner).

Select **Manage Notifications**.

Change the setting from **All Posts** or **Highlights** to **Off** (or **Friends' Posts** if you only want alerts from Facebook friends in that group).

Option 2: Turn off all Group Push Notifications globally

If you don't want push notifications from *any* Facebook groups:

Open Facebook and tap your **Profile / Menu icon** (bottom right on iOS).

Tap the **Gear icon** (Settings & Privacy -> **Settings**).

Scroll to **Preferences** and select **Notifications**.

Tap **Groups**.

Toggle off **Push** notifications (or set in-app notifications to **Off**).

Option 3: Disable directly from the Lock Screen / Notification Banner

If you're on iOS/macOS when the banner appears:

**iOS:** Swipe left on the notification banner, tap **Options**, then select **Turn Off** or **Manage Settings**.

---

## Assistant

Yeah, you've hit one of Facebook's most frustrating dark patterns. They deliberately omitted a global "Off" switch for Group Push Notifications.

If you want to kill these annoying algorithmic group highlights without manually opening every single group's page, you have two options:

1. The "Batch List" Screen (Fastest Way in App)

Instead of navigating into each group one by one, use Facebook’s bulk-editing list:

Tap **Menu** (bottom right) -> **Groups**.

Tap the **Gear Icon** (top right of the Groups screen).

Select **Notifications**.

This drops you into a single list of every group you belong to, where you can tap down the line and flip each to **Off**.

*(Note: Facebook has a known bug/quirk where flipping a toggle to "Off" sometimes reverts when you exit. Double-check that the setting stays "Off" after you tap it).*

2. Nuke it from the Notifications Tab

The next time one of these specific "Highlighted Post" push alerts lands in your Facebook bell icon tab:

Tap the **Notifications bell** inside the Facebook app.

Find the notification from that post and tap the **three dots (`...`)** on its right.

Select **Turn off notifications like this** or **Turn off notifications from this group**.

If you rarely use Facebook for real-time messages or time-sensitive alerts, disabling push notifications for the entire Facebook app in **iOS Settings -> Notifications -> Facebook** is often the cleanest resolution.

## Assistant

It really is absurd, but it's completely by design. Meta sets the default for every newly joined group to "Highlights" because their engagement algorithms rely on push notifications to pull users back into the app.

Because they intentionally don't provide a global default setting for groups, here are the only system-wide workarounds to avoid playing manual whack-a-mole:

Workaround 1: Silence Push Notifications OS-Wide

If you only use Facebook casually, turn off push notifications for the entire app at the OS level (**iOS Settings -> Notifications -> Facebook**). You'll still see in-app badges when you intentionally open the app, but it completely cuts off the push updates.

Workaround 2: Disable "Other Notifications" Category

While it won't fix existing groups, you can prevent Facebook's secondary algorithmic spam by going to **Settings & Privacy -> Settings -> Notifications**:

Scroll down to **Other Notifications** and toggle **Push** off.

Scroll to **More Activity About You** and turn off **Push**.

This stops a lot of the unprompted "A member posted in a group you haven't checked in a while" push alerts, even if individual group settings technically remain active.
