### 1. Tuition Deposit Reimbursement
* **Will you get reimbursed for the \$500 U of A tuition deposit?**
  * **Yes.** The \$500 admission/tuition deposit is not a fee; it is an advance payment credited directly to your official U of A Tuition & Student Fees ledger in Bear Tracks.
* **How the reconciliation works:**
  * When Alberta Student Aid confirms your full-time enrolment in late August / early September, Student Aid automatically remits the **entire assessed term tuition & mandatory academic fees** (~\$3,877.14 for 2 courses) directly to the university's account.
  * Because your \$500 deposit is already sitting on the ledger, Student Aid’s full payment creates an overpayment / negative balance of **\$500**.
  * The U of A Student Receivables office automatically refunds this credit balance directly into your linked direct-deposit bank account (in late September after the add/drop deadline).

---

### 2. Schedule 4 Doctor's Note / Medical Fee Reimbursement
* **Will you get reimbursed for the fee paid to your doctor for completing Schedule 4?**
  * **Yes.** Alberta Student Aid includes a specific **Medical Form Fee Reimbursement** provision (covering up to **\$3,500** for out-of-pocket costs paid to qualified medical doctors or psychoeducational assessors for disability verification paperwork).
* **How to claim it:**
  * Keep the itemized receipt from your physician's clinic showing the amount paid for completing the Disability Verification Form / Schedule 4.
  * Log into your **Alberta Student Aid** account, navigate to **Upload e-Documents**, select document type **"Disability Verification / Assessment Receipt"**, and upload a photo/PDF of the receipt.
  * The funds will be disbursed via direct deposit into your registered bank account.
