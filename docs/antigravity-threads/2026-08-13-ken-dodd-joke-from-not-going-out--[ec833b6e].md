Ah, the classic "next of grin" gag!
That line from Season 1, Episode 5 is pure Lee Mack paying tribute to his real-life hero. For those who don't have every frame of early *Not Going Out* permanently burned into their retinas:
**Lee:** "Did you hear Ken Dodd died?"
**Kate:** "No! Did he?"
**Lee:** "No, but his tickling stick has been left to his next of grin."
It’s the ultimate old-school, rapid-fire dad joke that fits Lee's character perfectly. Given that the real-life Ken Dodd was famous for playing five-hour stand-up sets that went well past 2:00 AM, there's a certain irony in delivering his style of humor in a quick, sharp sitcom beat.
