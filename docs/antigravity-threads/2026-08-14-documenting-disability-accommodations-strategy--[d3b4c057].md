# U of A: Disability, Schedule 4 & Accommodations

## 1. The 40% Reduced Course Load Full-Time Rule

Under the provisions of the **Canada Student Financial Assistance Program** and **Alberta Student Aid Policy**, any student with a medically verified **Permanent Disability** or **Persistent/Prolonged Disability** is entitled to take a **Reduced Course Load (40% to 59%)** while remaining legally classified as a **Full-Time Student**.

### Why This is the Linchpin of the Entire Degree Strategy:
- **Academic Pacing:** Taking 2 courses (6 credits) per semester instead of 5 courses eliminates academic burnout, allowing 100% focus on mastering complex Computer Science, Math, and AI topics.
- **Full-Time Funding Guaranteed:** Unlocks 100% of full-time living allowances ($1,791/mo standard allocation), full federal and provincial low-income grants, and specialized disability grants ($2,800 federal disability grant + up to $20,000 equipment/services fund).
- **Grade & Scholarship Protection:** Ensures the academic bandwidth required to maintain high marks (>90%) for continuing scholarships.

## 4. U of A Academic Success Centre / ARDS Onboarding Protocol

Upon accepting the offer of admission, execute the following onboarding steps:

1. **Intake Registration:** Submit medical documentation to **Accessibility Resources (ARDS)** within the U of A Academic Success Centre (`success@ualberta.ca`).
2. **Schedule 4 Institutional Sign-Off:** Have the ARDS Accessibility Advisor electronically sign/verify the educational institution section of the Schedule 4 to ensure Student Aid records are aligned.
3. **Formal Accommodation Letter:** Establish the following classroom accommodations for all Fall 2026 courses:
   - **Exam Accommodations:** 1.5× extra time in a private / reduced-distraction testing room.
   - **Auditory Accommodations:** Permission to use an FM wireless transmitter system or AI Live Captioning (e.g., Otter.ai / Whisper hardware).
   - **Assistive Technology Grant Application:** Apply for the **Canada Student Grant for Services and Equipment for Students with Disabilities (CSG-DSE)** (up to $20,000) for ergonomic seating, noise-canceling hardware, captioning software, and computing equipment.
