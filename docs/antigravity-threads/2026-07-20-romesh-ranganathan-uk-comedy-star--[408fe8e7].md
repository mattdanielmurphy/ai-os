Romesh Ranganathan is absolutely a massive star in the UK. In fact, he is often jokingly called "the hardest working man in comedy" because he is on British television and radio constantly. He spans a few different roles:

**Stand-up Comedian:** This is his primary root. He sells out massive nationwide arena tours, including multi-night stints at the O2 Arena in London, and has stand-up specials on Netflix.

**TV Presenter & Host:** He is the host of the BBC's high-profile revival of *The Weakest Link*, co-hosts the BAFTA TV Awards alongside Rob Beckett, and fronts several popular travelogues and game shows like *The Misadventures of Romesh Ranganathan* and *Parents' Evening*. He also took over the coveted Saturday lunchtime slot on BBC Radio 2.
