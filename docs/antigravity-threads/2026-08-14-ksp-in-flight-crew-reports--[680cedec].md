## Assistant

To take a Crew Report while in flight in Kerbal Space Program:

**Right-click on your Command Pod** (or Lander Can) where a Kerbal is currently seated.

Click **"Crew Report"** from the context menu.

In the science window that pops up, select either:

**Keep Data** (stores it in the pod to recover or transmit later).

**Transmit Data** (if you have an antenna and enough electric charge).

Pro Tips for Maximizing Science

**Free Up Space:** Command pods can only hold **one Crew Report at a time** by default. If you take a report in "Flying High" and want another in "Upper Atmosphere," transfer or transmit the first one.

**EVA Transfer:** If you don't have an antenna or power, send a Kerbal out on an EVA (even while floating/holding onto the ladder in safe flight), right-click the pod, select **"Take Data"**, and then board again. This moves the Crew Report into general experimental storage, allowing you to take a *new* Crew Report in the pod without overwriting the old one.

**Action Groups:** If you're using custom Action Groups in the VAB/SPH, you can bind "Crew Report" to a single key (like `1` or `2`) so you don't have to hunt and right-click during high-G re-entries or rapid atmospheric passes.
