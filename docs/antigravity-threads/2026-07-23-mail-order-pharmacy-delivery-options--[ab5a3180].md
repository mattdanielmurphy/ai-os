What This Document Is and What It Does

The Standards of Practice for Virtual Care sets strict legal limits on when Alberta pharmacies can use phone, internet, or mail-order delivery instead of treating patients in person.

## Assistant

To find open weight loss clinical trials—especially those covering new GLP-1/GIP compounds, triple-agonists (like retatrutide), or metabolic therapies where the drug and clinical care are provided at zero cost—there are **four main pipelines** in Alberta:

1. The Primary Database: ClinicalTrials.gov

Virtually all pharmaceutical-sponsored Phase 2 and Phase 3 trials (from Lilly, Novo Nordisk, Pfizer, etc.) must be registered here.

**How to search it:**

Go to ClinicalTrials.gov.

In **Condition/Disease**, type: `Obesity` or `Weight Loss`.

In **Location**, type: `Edmonton, Alberta, Canada` (or set a distance radius, e.g., 50 miles).

Filter **Status** to **Recruiting** or **Not yet recruiting**.

**What to look for in results:**

Look for trials evaluating **GIP/GLP-1**, **Glucagon/GIP/GLP-1 triple agonists**, or **oral GLP-1 small molecules**.

Check the **Eligibility Criteria** (typically BMI ≥30, or BMI ≥27 with a weight-related condition like hypertension).

Open the **Contacts and Locations** section to find local research coordinator emails or phone numbers at Edmonton study sites.

2. Alberta Diabetes Institute (University of Alberta)

The **Clinical Research Unit (CRU)** at the U of A is the primary academic hub in northern Alberta for metabolic, diabetes, and obesity clinical trials. They frequently run industry-sponsored and investigator-led trials.

**How to get on their radar:**

Visit the **Alberta Diabetes Institute - Participate in a Study** page (`ualberta.ca/alberta-diabetes/clinical-research`).

Fill out their **Participant Database Form**. This adds you to a confidential registry where coordinators email you directly when a new trial opens that matches your profile (age, BMI, health history).

Browse their **"Currently Recruiting"** tab under the *Obesity* or *Nutrition & Metabolism* categories.

3. Dedicated Clinical Research Sites in Edmonton

In addition to the university, private clinical research organizations (CROs) conduct Phase 2–4 pharmaceutical trials for big pharma in the Edmonton area. These sites recruit directly from the community.

Key local research centers to check or contact:

**Northern Alberta Clinical Trials Center (NACTC)** / **UAlberta Clinical Trials Office**: Handles trial site listings across local hospitals (Royal Alexandra, University of Alberta Hospital).

**Private Clinical Trial Sites:** Search for private sites in Edmonton like *Synergy Respiratory & Clinical Research*, *Leduc Clinical Research*, or local outpatient clinical research groups that run pharma-sponsored obesity trials. Calling their recruitment lines directly puts you on their callbacks list.

Canadian non-profit organizations maintain centralized patient recruitment portals specifically for bariatric and metabolic studies across Canada.

**Obesity Matters Canada:** Has a dedicated clinical trial connector (`[obesity-matters.com/clinical-trials/](https://obesity-matters.com/clinical-trials/)`) that lists active Canadian trials and helps match patients with trial coordinators.

Tips for Screening & Applying

**Be mindful of the "Washout Period":** Most GLP-1/tirzepatide trials require you to have been off any anti-obesity or diabetes injectable medication (like Mounjaro or Ozempic) for **at least 30 to 90 days** prior to screening. If you are currently taking Mounjaro, mention this right away to the study coordinator to ask about their washout window.

**Placebo Risk:** Always ask the study coordinator about the **randomization ratio** (e.g., 1:1 or 2:1 active drug vs. placebo). In Phase 3 trials, you have a high probability of receiving the active study drug, but there is always a chance of receiving a placebo or an active comparator.

**What is covered:** Standard clinical trials cover 100% of the study medication, lab work, physical exams, and clinical visits, and many offer small stipends or travel/parking reimbursements for each clinic visit.
