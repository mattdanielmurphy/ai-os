## Assistant

The absolute cheapest way to get to Europe (and eventually the UK) from Edmonton comes down to **two main strategies**: flying via Iceland as a budget gateway, or repositioning to Calgary for direct transatlantic sales.

Strategy 1: The Edmonton–Reykjavík Gateway (Cheapest Base Airfare)

**Icelandair** operates direct, non-stop flights from Edmonton (YEG) to Reykjavík (KEF).

**Leg 1: Edmonton (YEG) → Reykjavík (KEF)**

**Base Fare:** As low as **$290–$350 CAD** one-way off-peak (fall/winter/spring, non-stop ~6.5 hours).

**Leg 2: Reykjavík (KEF) → UK (London, Manchester, or Edinburgh)**

**Base Fare:** Connecting on ultra-low-cost carriers (easyJet, PLAY, Wizz Air, or Ryanair) from KEF to UK airports costs between **$50–$130 CAD** (£30–£80).

**Total Cost:** ~**$340–$480 CAD** one-way.

**Pro-Tip:** Icelandair allows a free stopover in Iceland for up to 7 days on single-ticket bookings (e.g., YEG → KEF → LHR/EDI) without adding extra airfare, which usually totals **$450–$550 CAD** on one itinerary.

Strategy 2: Calgary (YYC) Bus Positioning + Direct Transatlantic Flight

Calgary is WestJet’s primary international hub and gets significantly more direct, high-volume transatlantic competition than YEG.

**Ground Transport:** Take **Ebus, Red Arrow, or FlixBus** from Edmonton to Calgary Airport/City ($45–$70 CAD).

**Flight:** Catch a direct WestJet or Air Transat flight from YYC to **London Gatwick (LGW)**, **London Heathrow (LHR)**, **Dublin (DUB)**, or **Edinburgh (EDI)**.

**Total Cost:** On promotional sales (typically Sept–Nov and Jan–April), one-way fares out of YYC to LGW/EDI drop to **$350–$450 CAD**. Combined with ground transit, total cost is roughly **$400–$500 CAD**.

Strategy 3: Standard 1-Stop Connections (YEG → YYZ/YUL → UK)

If you prefer a single ticket with protected transfers and baggage handling directly from YEG:

**Airlines:** WestJet, Air Canada, or Air Transat.

**Connecting Hubs:** Toronto Pearson (YYZ) or Montréal–Trudeau (YUL).

**Cost:** Single-ticket standard economy fares off-peak range from **$480–$550 CAD** one-way to London, Dublin, or Manchester.

Key Tactics to Keep Costs at the Absolute Minimum

**Personal Item Only (Backpack):** Lowest tier base fares (Icelandair *Economy Light*, WestJet *UltraBasic*) do not include overhead carry-on bags or checked luggage. Adding a cabin bag can cost $50–$90 extra per leg. Packing strictly in an under-seat personal item keeps the quoted base fare real.

**Travel Days & Seasonality:** Fly on **Tuesdays or Wednesdays** in shoulder seasons (September to mid-December, or mid-January to April). Summer (June–August) and holiday windows double or triple these prices.

**Points Redemption (Aeroplan):** If using Aeroplan points, off-peak one-way flights from YEG to Western Europe start at **35,000 points + ~$70–$100 CAD** in taxes/fees.
