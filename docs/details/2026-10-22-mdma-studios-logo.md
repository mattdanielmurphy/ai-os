## 11:45 Logo directions

What Matt asked: logo, small brand kit and app/menu-bar icon for MDMA Studios; concept is a tablet that mimics a stamped pill. Show 2–3 directions first.

---

### Three directions

- A Scored tablet: round chalk tablet, score line splits "MD" / "MA". Pine green #1F3A33 + signal red #E2553B. Recommended: the name split by the score line is the joke, read instantly.
- B Caplet: coral oblong pill, "MD|MA" with a vertical score. Cream #F2ECE0 + coral #D6683F. Menu-bar icon is a wide pill outline.
- C Command pill: ⌘ debossed into a mint tablet. Graphite #1C2023 + sage #5E9C7C. Best menu-bar legibility, least "MDMA".
- Type: Futura Bold (logo, built into macOS) + Avenir Next (UI/sub-labels).

---

### Menu bar caveat

A's "MD/MA" letters blur at the real 22pt size. If A wins, the menu-bar icon drops the letters and keeps just the scored disc (or uses ⌘ from C).

---

### How it's made

SVG generated by assets/brand/directions/gen.py, rendered with rsvg-convert. Figma connector works (whoami OK); the chosen direction gets built in Figma after Matt picks. Rejected: building drafts in Figma first (slower, starter-plan rate limits). Undo: delete assets/brand/.
