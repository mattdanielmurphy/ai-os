# Team plan vs shared repos

## 16:05 Is Claude Team worth it for Matt and Tyler?

Matt and Tyler were debating on a live call whether a Claude Team plan gives "25% more usage". Matt thought it's 25% more usage for 25% more money.

---

### Matt is right on monthly billing

From [claude.com/pricing](https://claude.com/pricing), checked 2026-10-27:

- Team Premium seat: $125/month billed monthly, $100/month billed annually. Premium = 5x a Standard seat.
- Team Standard seat: $25 monthly / $20 annual, "more usage than Pro" (blogs say 1.25x Pro; Anthropic's page doesn't give the number).
- So Premium ≈ 6.25x Pro if the 1.25x figure holds.
- Max 5x: $100/month, monthly only, 5x Pro.
- Monthly: $125 for ~6.25x vs $100 for 5x = 25% more usage for 25% more money. Same rate.
- Annual: $100 for ~6.25x = 25% more usage for the same money, but you're locked in for a year.
- Team minimum is 2 seats, so you two qualify. Team also includes up to $500/month pooled API credits (Max 5x includes $100).

---

### What Team adds for working together

- Shared projects (both of you in the same Claude project, its threads and files).
- Org-wide skills deployment and admin-managed connectors.
- That's it for collaboration. Code, CLAUDE.md, .rules and skills already sync through the slub-hq GitHub repo, which works with any AI.

---

### Recommendation

Stay on Max 5x and keep syncing through slub-hq for now. Team only pays off if you go annual (25% more usage, same price) and actually want shared Claude projects. Tyler already dropped Teams on 2026-10-26 to avoid Claude lock-in (see 2026-10-26-slub-sync-with-tyler.md, "Slack vs Telegram").
