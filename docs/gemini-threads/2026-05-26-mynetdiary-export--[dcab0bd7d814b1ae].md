## Gemini

MyNetDiary does not offer a single "all-time" export button for your entire history, but you can export your data in yearly chunks using their web interface.


How to Export Your Data

* Log in to the MyNetDiary website on a desktop browser.

* Navigate to the Analysis tab.

* Look for a Data Export link, typically located in the bottom left-hand side of the page.

* From there, you can select the specific year you wish to download, and the data will be provided as an Excel/CSV file.


Important Notes

* Yearly Basis: You will need to repeat this process for each year of data you want to retrieve.

* Daily Notes: If you are trying to export your "Daily Notes," there isn't a direct export function for them. The most common workaround is to generate a PDF report within the app (under Daily Analysis), which includes your notes at the bottom of the summary.

* Automated Alternatives: Since you are comfortable with command-line tools and Python, there are community-maintained scripts on GitHub (such as talwrii/data_mynetdiary) designed to scrape and export your MyNetDiary weight and food data into a more usable CSV format. Given your background in automation, you might find this approach more efficient than manual downloads if you have several years of logs.
