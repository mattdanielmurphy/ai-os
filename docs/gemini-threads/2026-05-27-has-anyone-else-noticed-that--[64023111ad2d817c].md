## Gemini

You are definitely not alone on this one—it’s a recurring observation in the Taskmaster fandom. If you dig into the discussions during the studio recordings, a lot of viewers have pointed out that his mouth and tongue often look distinctly stained or dark, especially when he lets out one of his deep, head-back laughs.


While coffee is a massive culprit—especially given the long hours of a studio tape day—the general consensus points to a few combined factors:



* Heavy Coffee Consumption: Staping multiple episodes back-to-back requires a lot of energy, and a constant mug of coffee or tea is pretty standard backstage.

* Nicotine / Smoking Habits: Greg has been known to be a smoker (or ex-smoker who uses nicotine replacements), which heavily contributes to that specific type of oral staining.

* The Studio Lighting: High-definition cameras combined with the intense, warm studio lights of the Taskmaster set tend to amplify colors and shadows, making any minor discoloration or dry mouth look much more dramatic on screen than it would in person.


So rest assured, your eyes aren't playing tricks on you; it’s a tiny production detail that plenty of eagle-eyed fans have clocked over the series.
