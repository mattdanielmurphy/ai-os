## Gemini

Based on your screenshots, your Aggregate Device is configured correctly in macOS, but Logic Pro likely needs a quick adjustment in its I/O Assignments to treat "Stereo Output" and "Output 3-4" as distinct physical destinations.


Right now, Logic is likely defaulting both to the first two channels of your aggregate device. Here is how to fix the routing:


1. Update Logic's Output Assignments
Even if you select "Output 3-4" on a track, Logic needs to know that "Output 3-4" corresponds to your iRig.



* Go to Settings (or Preferences) > Audio > I/O Assignments.

* Look at the Output tab.

* Check the Stereo Output setting. It is likely set to Output 1-2.

* If you want to use a second stereo bus for your iRig, you can either:

* Change the routing of a specific Bus to Output 3-4.

* Ensure that your Master Output isn't mirroring all signals to 1-2.





2. Verify the Channel Mapping
Looking at your Audio MIDI Setup screenshot:



* Channels 1-2 (UMC22 L/R): This is your Behringer/USB Codec.

* Channels 3-4 (iRig L/R): This is your iRig Pre HD.


In Logic, if you have a track set to Output 3-4, it should only be hitting the iRig. If you still hear it through the Behringer, check your Stereo Out channel strip in the Mixer. If you have multiple tracks and some are set to "Stereo Out" and others to "Output 3-4," make sure "Stereo Out" isn't being used as a global "sum" that includes 3-4.


3. Check Clock Source & Drift
I see you have Drift Correction enabled for the iRig. This is good, as it keeps the two interfaces in sync. However, if the audio is "bleeding" between them, it’s almost always a software routing issue rather than a hardware one.



Quick Test:



* Create two Mono tracks.

* Set Track 1 to Output 1. (Should only come out of the Behringer).

* Set Track 2 to Output 3. (Should only come out of the iRig).

* If Track 2 still comes out of the Behringer, check Mixer > Master Section to ensure there isn't a "Multi-Output" or "Mirroring" setting active in your Audio Settings.


Are you trying to use these for two different sets of monitors, or is one for a specific outboard gear loop?
