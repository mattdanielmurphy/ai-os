## Gemini

Celebrity Island with Bear Grylls is a British reality television spin-off of The Island with Bear Grylls. Broadcast on Channel 4 in the UK between 2016 and 2018, the series was produced in aid of Cancer Research UK’s Stand Up to Cancer campaign.


The premise strips famous personalities of their luxury lifestyles, fending for themselves on a remote, uninhabited Pacific island with minimal tools, basic survival training, and limited initial water rations. They must secure their own food, water, and shelter while entirely filming the experience themselves.



Series Overview

* Series 1 (2016): 10 celebrities marooned for two weeks.

* Series 2 (2017): The duration was extended to four weeks.

* Series 3 (2018): Another four-week stint featuring a mix of intense conditions, survival struggles, and internal camp conflicts.



Notable Contestants & Occurrences
The harsh tropical environments, torrential rains, and severe lack of food frequently pushed the participants to their absolute physical and mental limits, leading to several high-profile departures:



* Series 1: Former JLS singer Aston Merrygold and former rugby player Thom Evans both left early due to the brutal reality of the environment and lack of food.

* Series 2: Actor RJ Mitte (Breaking Bad) walked out after a heavy storm, expressing frustration with the group's lack of organization. Comedian Mark Watson suffered severe chest pains and insomnia, leaving during the final episode.

* Series 3: Actress Roxanne Pallett left within the first few days following a panic attack triggered by campfire smoke. The Only Way Is Essex star Pete Wicks made headlines for catching a nurse shark in a net, which bit his finger, and for a moral stance against eating a pig that the group encountered.
