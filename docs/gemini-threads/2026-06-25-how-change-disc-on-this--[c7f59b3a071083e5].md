Changing the disc on a Dremel Saw-Max (SM20) requires a specific trick because the arbor bolt is reverse-threaded.


Here is the step-by-step process to swap the disc:


1. Prep and Safety

* Unplug the tool: Always disconnect the power cord before changing blades or discs.

* Locate the Spindle Lock: This is the black button located on top of the tool near the gear housing (just above the blue plastic accents).


2. Remove the Old Disc

* Press and hold down the Spindle Lock button to keep the internal shaft from spinning.

* Insert the Allen/hex wrench (which came with the tool) into the center lock bolt.

* Turn the wrench CLOCKWISE to loosen the bolt. (Remember: Righty-loosey, lefty-tighty in this case!)

* Remove the lock bolt and the small outer washer. Slide the old wheel out from behind the guard.


3. Install the New Disc

* Slide your new metal cut-off wheel (e.g., the SM510 metal wheel) behind the guard and seat it flat against the inner washer on the shaft.

* Replace the outer washer and thread the lock bolt back in by hand.

* Hold down the Spindle Lock button again.

* Use the wrench to tighten the bolt by turning it COUNTER-CLOCKWISE. Ensure it is snug, but do not over-torque it.



Tips for Shaving the Door Latch
Since you are adjusting a metal door latch:



* Use the SM510 Metal Cut-off Wheel: Make sure you aren't using a wood or masonry wheel, as they won't cut or grind metal safely.

* Secure the workspace: If you're removing the latch plate from the door, clamp it tightly to a workbench. If you're grinding it while it's still attached to the door frame, proceed with light, shallow passes to avoid over-shaving or throwing heavy sparks into the door frame.

* Watch your depth: Adjust the foot on the Saw-Max using the depth adjustment lever so only a small portion of the wheel extends past the guard, giving you maximum control for a clean, precise grind.

* Wear eye protection: Grinding metal generates fast-flying sparks.
