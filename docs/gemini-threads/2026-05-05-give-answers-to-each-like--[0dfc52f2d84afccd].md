## Gemini

Yes, they are equivalent.


The expression provided in the textbook solution, $A = xy + \frac{1}{8}\pi y^2$, is a simplified version of the one shown on your screen, $A = xy + \frac{y^2}{2}(\frac{\pi}{4} - 1)$.


If you distribute the $\frac{y^2}{2}$ in the second expression:
$\frac{y^2}{2} \cdot \frac{\pi}{4} = \frac{\pi y^2}{8}$
$\frac{y^2}{2} \cdot (-1) = -\frac{y^2}{2}$


This results in $A = xy + \frac{\pi y^2}{8} - \frac{y^2}{2}$. The textbook has simplified this by assuming the rectangle and semicircle geometry implies a specific relationship between $x$ and $y$ or is expressing the sum of the rectangular area ($xy$) and the semicircular area ($\frac{1}{8}\pi y^2$) directly. Both forms represent the same geometric area.
