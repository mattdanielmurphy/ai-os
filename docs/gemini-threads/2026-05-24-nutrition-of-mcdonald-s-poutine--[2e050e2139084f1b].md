## Gemini

Your instinct is spot-on. It isn't a traditional, slow-simmered pan gravy made from pan drippings and a flour roux.


Instead, McDonald’s uses a highly engineered, powder-based reconstituted gravy thickened with starches rather than flour.


What's actually in the gravy?
The base flavor profile relies on hydrolyzed corn protein, yeast extract, and chicken broth powder/fat for that savory, umami punch.


Instead of a flour roux, they use modified corn and tapioca starches to achieve that glossy, thick texture that holds up under heat lamps. It also uses tomato and onion powders for depth, alongside caramel color to give it that deep brown look.


Because it's thickened with corn and tapioca starch instead of wheat flour, the gravy itself contains no gluten.



The Nutrition Breakdown
When you combine those famous fries, real white cheddar cheese curds, and a heavy ladle of that starchy gravy, the numbers stack up quickly.


The official breakdown for a regular serving (254g) includes:

The Sodium Spike: The most intense stat here is the sodium. At 3,170 mg, a single regular poutine blows past your entire recommended daily sodium intake (2,300 mg) in one sitting, mostly driven by the combination of the salted fries, the curd brine, and the powder-based gravy mix.
