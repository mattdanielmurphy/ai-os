## Gemini

In Fort Saskatchewan, the rush hour window typically runs from 3:30 PM to 6:00 PM. By leaving at 5:00 PM, you would be entering the heart of the peak traffic volume, which is the exact opposite of what you want for a controlled, low-stress drive.


The Timing Strategy
You want the "sweet spot" where traffic is thinned out but visibility remains high.



* The Best Window: 7:00 PM to 8:30 PM.

* Why: * Traffic: By 7:00 PM, the commuter flow has largely dissipated. You will encounter significantly fewer stop-and-go situations.

* Light: We are in early May, so sunset in your area is around 9:00 PM. You will have plenty of daylight to maintain perfect visibility, which helps you scan far ahead for red lights and obstacles.

* The "Coasting" Advantage: With fewer cars on the road, you are less likely to be forced into an emergency stop or a sharp acceleration by someone cutting you off or tailgating you.





Coordination with your Dad
If your dad finishes work at 5:00 PM, he is likely looking to settle in for the evening. If you push the drive to 7:00 PM:



* Lower Stress: He won't be stressed by the rush hour traffic either, meaning he’ll be more relaxed as your chase car/ride back.

* Safety: You aren't forcing the Yaris through a swarm of commuters who might be impatient with you if you're taking your time.


Why this changes the "Mechanical" Plan
Waiting until 7:00 PM is a huge tactical advantage.



* Zero pressure: You don't have to worry about people tailgating you or rushing your gear changes.

* Temperature: The ambient air temperature will be slightly cooler in the evening than at 5:00 PM, which is a minor but real benefit for engine and transmission cooling.
