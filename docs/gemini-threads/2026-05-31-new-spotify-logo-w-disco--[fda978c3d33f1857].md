## Gemini

No, it's not permanent.


Spotify officially confirmed that the photorealistic disco ball icon is just a temporary look to celebrate the company’s 20th anniversary and roll out their "Party of the Year(s)" feature.


After catching a massive wave of backlash from users who found the skeuomorphic, shiny design jarring on their home screens, Spotify posted a statement clarifying that the party is short-lived:



"Alright, we know glitter is not for everyone. Our temp glow up ends soon. Your regularly scheduled Spotify icon returns next week."



The rollout began in mid-May, and the app icon is already reverting back to the standard flat green logo for users as they update to the latest version.

## Gemini

Mounjaro delays gastric emptying, meaning high-fat or heavy meals (like bacon and ham) sit in your stomach much longer than usual. This frequently triggers nausea, indigestion, or a heavy, upset stomach.


While you have to wait for the meal to digest, here are a few immediate steps to help manage the discomfort:


Immediate Relief Steps

* Stay Upright: Do not lie down for at least 2 to 3 hours after eating. Lying flat makes it easier for stomach acid and undigested food to move up, worsening nausea and reflux.

* Sip, Don't Gulp: Drink small amounts of clear fluids. Cold water, clear broth, or iced peppermint/ginger tea can help soothe the stomach lining. Avoid carbonated drinks, which add gas to an already crowded stomach.

* Gentle Movement: A slow, easy walk can help stimulate digestion. Avoid strenuous exercise or bending over.

* OTC Relief:

* Simethicone (Gas-X): If you feel bloated or trapped gas.

* Antacids (Tums, Mylanta) or H2 Blockers (Pepcid): If you are experiencing heartburn or acid reflux.





For the Next 24 Hours

* Pivot to Bland Foods: When you feel ready to eat again, stick to small amounts of easily digestible items like toast, crackers, rice, or applesauce.

* Prioritize Hydration: Mounjaro increases the risk of dehydration when you aren't eating or drinking normally. Focus on getting electrolytes if you experience any vomiting or diarrhea.


Managing Future Meals on Mounjaro
Because the medication significantly alters how your body processes food, your tolerance for certain macros changes. Moving forward:



* Watch the Fats: High-fat items (fatty meats, heavy oils, fried foods) are the most common trigger for Mounjaro-induced GI distress.

* Stop Before You're Full: Because of the delayed emptying, the signal that you are full often arrives late. Eating smaller, more frequent portions helps prevent overloading the stomach.


If your stomach pain becomes severe, or if you experience persistent vomiting and cannot keep fluids down, reach out to your prescribing doctor or a healthcare professional.
