## Gemini

The combination of Mounjaro and a heavy night of drinking can lead to a uniquely rough morning.


Mounjaro slows down your stomach emptying. Because alcohol sits in your stomach much longer instead of moving quickly into your intestines, it has more time to irritate your gastric lining. This delay is a primary driver for the intense hangover and severe heartburn you are experiencing.



Is Beer Acidic?
Yes, beer is highly acidic.


Most standard beers have a pH level between 4.0 and 5.0 (with some sours dropping as low as 3.0). For context, neutral water is 7.0, and anything below that is acidic.


When you drink beer, two things trigger your heartburn:



* The Acidity and Carbonation: The natural acid and carbon dioxide bubbles irritate the stomach.

* The Alcohol Effect: Alcohol relaxes the lower esophageal sphincter—the muscular valve that keeps stomach acid from creeping up into your throat. Combine that relaxed valve with Mounjaro’s slow digestion, and acid has a clear path upward.



What to Do Right Now
Because your digestion is working in slow motion today, your recovery strategy needs to focus on gentle hydration and acid management.





A Note on Pain Relief: If you have a headache, stick to acetaminophen (Tylenol) if your liver is up for it, but use a conservative dose since you drank. Avoid NSAIDs like ibuprofen (Advil) or aspirin today, as they are highly corrosive to an already irritated stomach lining and will make your heartburn worse.
