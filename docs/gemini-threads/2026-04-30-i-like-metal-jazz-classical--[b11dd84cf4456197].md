## Gemini

If the 7 and 9 pairing method is stalling out, it usually means the remote is still holding onto a "stuck" Bluetooth connection or the box's pairing cache needs a refresh.


Before unplugging the box, try a Factory Reset of the remote itself. This often clears the pairing loop where the TV says "Pairing" but never finishes.


1. Factory Reset the Remote

* Press and hold 1 and 9 together for about 3 seconds until the LED light turns green.

* Press the OK button.

* The light should flash green twice and then turn white. This confirms the remote is now in a "clean" state.


2. Restart the Digital Box
Since the TV is stuck on the "Pairing" screen, a power cycle is a good idea to reset the box's Bluetooth receiver:



* Unplug the power cable from the back of the TELUS TV Digital Box.

* Wait 30 seconds.

* Plug it back in and let the box fully reboot to the home screen.


3. Re-pair the Remote
Once the box is back on:



* Point the remote at the box.

* Press and hold 7 and 9 again for 3 seconds.

* The light should flash green twice when it successfully connects.


If it still fails:



* Check the batteries: Even if the LED blinks, low voltage can cause the Bluetooth pairing handshake to fail. Try a fresh pair of AAAs.

* Manual Menu Path: If you have another remote (or a USB keyboard/mouse you can plug into the box) that works, you can navigate to Settings > Device settings > Remotes and Accessories > Program Remote with TV to force the pairing process from the software side.
