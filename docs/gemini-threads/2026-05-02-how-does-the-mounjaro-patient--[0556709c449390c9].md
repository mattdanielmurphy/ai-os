## Gemini

The Mounjaro Patient Support Program (PSP) in Canada is designed to reduce the out-of-pocket cost for patients who do not have private insurance coverage.


1. Enrollment Process
You do not give your personal details to Costco to start the program; you must enroll yourself or be enrolled by your doctor first.



* Self-Enrollment: Visit mymounjaro.ca. You will need to provide your basic contact information and potentially the name of your prescribing physician.

* The "Card": Once registered, you will receive a digital or printable Patient Support Card. This card contains three critical pieces of information for the pharmacist:

* Group Number

* Carrier ID

* Patient ID (Certificate Number)





2. At the Costco Pharmacy
When you bring your 15 mg prescription to the Costco pharmacy counter:



* Hand over the PSP Card: Give the pharmacist the codes from your MyMounjaro registration.

* Explain the Payor Status: Tell the pharmacist you are "paying out of pocket" and that this is a "Secondary Payor" or "Co-pay Card."

* Adjudication: The pharmacist enters the codes into their system (Kroll or similar pharmacy software). The system "bills" the PSP as if it were an insurance company.

* The Discount: The system will automatically calculate the discount. For cash-paying patients in Canada, this typically reduces the price by 20% to 25% (bringing a ~$450 pen down to approximately $340–$360).


3. Using the 15 mg Pen with the PSP
The program generally applies to any dose of Mounjaro, including the 15 mg KwikPen.



* Quantity Limits: The PSP usually covers one pen (a 28-day supply) every 21 to 25 days. If you are using the "splitting" method to make a 15 mg pen last for 12 weeks, you will only be using the discount once every three months.

* Prescription Match: The pharmacy system must see a prescription for "Mounjaro 15 mg" to apply the discount to a 15 mg pen. If your prescription is for 5 mg but you want to buy the 15 mg pen to split it, the pharmacist cannot legally dispense the 15 mg version or apply the discount unless the doctor updates the script to the higher dose.


Why Costco?
Costco is often recommended for this specific medication because:



* Lower Markup: They generally have the lowest base price for the KwikPen in Alberta before the discount is even applied.

* Dispensing Fees: Their dispensing fees are typically around $4.49, compared to $12–$14 at other major chains.

* Familiarity: Because so many people pay out of pocket for GLP-1 medications at Costco, their pharmacists are usually very efficient at processing PSP cards.


Summary Checklist

* Get a prescription for Mounjaro 15 mg KwikPen from your doctor.

* Register at mymounjaro.ca to get your certificate/card.

* Take both to the pharmacy.

* Ensure they apply the PSP as the primary/sole coverage (since you have no other insurance).

* Purchase 32G 4mm pen needles separately (they are not covered by the PSP).
