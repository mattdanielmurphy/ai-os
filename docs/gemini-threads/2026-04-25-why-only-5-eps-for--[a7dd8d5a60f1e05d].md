## Gemini

In the early days, Taskmaster was still finding its footing and didn't have the massive budget or the established "10-episode" standard it has today.


The short run for Series 2 (and Series 3) was mainly due to:



* Risk Management: When the show began on the Dave channel, it was a relatively experimental and risky format. The network initially ordered shorter runs to see if the success of the first series (which had 6 episodes) would continue.

* Back-to-Back Production: Series 2 and 3 were produced closely together and aired in the same year (2016). This fast turnaround meant the production team had to work with the resources and tasks they had available at the time.

* UK Television Standards: It is very common for UK shows—especially new ones—to start with short, 6-episode "pilot" series. Taskmaster actually dropped to 5 episodes for Series 2 and 3 before the order was bumped up to 8 episodes for Series 4 and 5, finally settling on the current 10-episode format from Series 6 onward.


Essentially, they didn't yet know it would become the juggernaut it is now, so they were filming as many high-quality tasks as their early budget and schedule allowed.
