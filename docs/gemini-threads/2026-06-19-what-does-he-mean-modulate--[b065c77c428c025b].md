## Gemini

When the host is speaking around 26:56, he is actually saying "modulate a major second" (the musical interval) rather than "an A-Maj."


Here is what he means in that context:


The Musical Explanation

* Modulation: In music, modulation just means changing from one key to another within a piece of music.

* A Major Second: A major second is an interval of a whole step (or two semitones)—for example, shifting a musical pattern from the key of C up to the key of D.


Context in the Video
The hosts are discussing the song "Real Love" from the Swans album Children of God. The speaker explains that he dislikes a specific songwriting trope common in blues and rock: taking a single foundational riff, playing it in one key, and then simply shifting the entire pattern up a whole step (a major second) to play the exact same thing in a new key.


To his ears, repeating this specific back-and-forth structural shift makes the track feel predictable or lazy, which is why he calls it the album's only "stinker."
