---

## Gemini

For digital delivery from electronic transcript services (like Parchment or other digital sending networks used by Ontario institutions), MacEwan University requires documents to be sent to their primary admissions email:


admissions@macewan.ca


A Few Quick Things to Keep in Mind:

* Direct Delivery: Make sure the service sends it directly to that email. MacEwan only considers transcripts official if they come straight from the issuing school or digital platform, rather than being forwarded by you.

* Keep Tracking: Once sent, it can take a bit for their systems to pair the transcript with your file. You can monitor the status under your To Do List by logging into myPortal.MacEwan.ca and checking the Admissions & Transfer Credit section in myStudentSystem.

## Gemini

Yes, this comes directly from MacEwan University's official documentation regarding document and transcript submission.


According to their guidelines for institutions using electronic credential systems (such as Parchment or the National Student Clearinghouse):



"Many institutions send documents electronically using services like Parchment and National Student Clearing House. If documents are being sent digitally, they must be sent to admissions@macewan.ca."



They also note that if you have general tracking questions after it is sent, or need to clarify something with the Registrar's office, you can contact transcripts@macewan.ca or info@macewan.ca. However, for the initial automated/digital intake from an outside province, the standard target address to provide your sender is admissions@macewan.ca.
