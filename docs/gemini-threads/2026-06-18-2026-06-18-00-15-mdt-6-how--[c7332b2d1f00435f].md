## Gemini

To view two completely separate execution outputs (like your macOS app console and an iOS/iPadOS simulator console) side by side in Xcode, you can split the Console / Debug Area into two distinct panes.


Here is exactly how to do it:


Step 1: Split the Debug Console

* Look at the very bottom right corner of your console panel (the area where the log text is scrolling).

* You will see a small layout icon that looks like a square split vertically or a tiny + icon depending on your exact version window controls, typically right next to the console clear button (trash can).

* Alternatively, click anywhere inside the console log text to focus it, then go to the top menu bar and select Editor > Split Console (or look for Create Console Pane).


This will split your bottom drawer into two parallel text windows.



Step 2: Route the Correct Process to Each Pane
Once you have two panes open, you can assign each one to look at a different target or destination terminal stream:



* At the top of each individual split pane, there is a small header bar with a dropdown menu.

* Click that dropdown in the left pane and select your StudyEngine_macOS process.

* Click the dropdown in the right pane and select your iPad target or secondary active debug process thread.


Now, as both targets execute or log data via print statements, you can track their outputs concurrently without constantly toggling the target destination dropdown in the top toolbar.
