It is a classic case of crossword wordplay involving a bit of visual trickery.


In this clue, "control center" isn't referring to a headquarters or a command hub. Instead, it is asking you to look at the literal center of the word "control."


The Breakdown
If you look at the spelling of the word CONTROL:



* C-O-N (first three letters)

* T (the middle letter)

* R-O-L (last three letters)


The letter at the very center of the word "control" is T. In crosswords, when a letter is used as an answer, it is often spelled out phonetically. Therefore, the letter T becomes TEE.


The question mark at the end of the clue is the dead giveaway that the constructor is using a pun or literal interpretation rather than a standard definition.
