To prevent burning, you have two options: lower the temperature or use a physical shield. Lowering the temp is usually easiest.

* Time: 3 to 5 minutes.

* Temperature: 375°F (190°C).

* Time: 5 to 8 minutes.


The Steps

* Preheat your oven completely.

* Place on a baking sheet: Wrap the bottom and sides of the sub in foil, but leave the top completely open so the cheese can melt and the exposed edges of the bread can crisp up.
