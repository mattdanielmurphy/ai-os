## Gemini

To chain effects together using the MindMeld MixMaster and AuxSpander in VCV Rack 2, you have two main approaches depending on whether you want the chained effects to be shared globally across multiple channels (using the AuxSpander) or dedicated to a single channel.


Here is how to set up both configurations.



Method 1: Chaining Effects Globally (via AuxSpander)
If you want to send multiple mixer channels into the same sequence of effects (e.g., sending a synth and a drum track through a chorus then a delay), you chain the effects in series within the AuxSpander's send/return loop.


The Routing Logic

* AuxSpander Send → Effect 1 Input: Connect the AUX SEND output (1-4) from the AuxSpander into the input of your first effect module.

* Effect 1 Output → Effect 2 Input: Connect the audio output of the first effect directly into the input of the second effect. You can repeat this for as many modules as you want in the chain.

* Final Effect Output → AuxSpander Return: Connect the output of the last effect module in your chain back into the corresponding AUX RETURN inputs on the AuxSpander.


How to Mix It

* Set the Dry/Wet mix to 100% Wet on all effect modules in the chain. Because the audio is running through them sequentially, you want the full signal passing to the next stage.

* Use the corresponding AUX knob on your MixMaster channel strips to control how much of that channel's signal gets sent into the entire FX chain.

* Use the Aux Return knob at the top of the AuxSpander (or the master return section) to control the overall volume of the combined wet effects back into the master mix.



Method 2: Chaining Effects on a Single Channel (Insert Chain)
If you want a chain of effects to apply exclusively to one specific instrument channel on the MixMaster, you don't actually need the AuxSpander. Instead, you utilize the Direct Out or Inserts of that specific channel.


The Routing Logic

* MixMaster Channel Direct Out → Effect 1 Input: Take the DIR OUT (Direct Output) from the bottom of the specific MixMaster channel and plug it into the first effect's input.

* Effect 1 Output → Effect 2 Input: Patch the output of Effect 1 into Effect 2.

* Final Effect Output → Next Destination: * If you want to return the treated signal back to a fresh MixMaster channel for leveling, plug the final effect output into the IN of an unused MixMaster channel.

* Note: If you do this, make sure to unroute or mute the original channel's main mix send if you only want to hear the wet/processed signal.






Quick Troubleshooting Tips

* Phase/Phasing issues: If your chain sounds thin or hollow, ensure that all effects in an AuxSpander send/return loop are set to 100% wet. If any dry signal leaks through the effects in series, it can cause phase cancellation when mixed back with the original dry channel on the MixMaster.

* Stereo vs. Mono: MixMaster and AuxSpander handle stereo pairs. If an effect module in your chain is mono-only, duplicating the signal or using a mono-to-stereo utility module before hitting the next stereo effect will keep your spatial image intact.
