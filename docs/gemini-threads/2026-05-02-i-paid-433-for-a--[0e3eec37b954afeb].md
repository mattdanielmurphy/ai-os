How to tell for sure
Check your pharmacy receipt for these indicators:

It is very common for the person at the "drop-off" window to scan the card but for the "adjudication" (the actual digital handshake with Lilly's system) to fail or not be completed before the "pick-up" person rings you out.

What you should do now

Most Alberta pharmacies (especially Costco) can "reverse and re-bill" a transaction if it was within the last 7–14 days.



* Bring your original receipt and your Mounjaro PSP card (the one with the Group, Carrier, and Patient ID).

* Tell them: "I provided my Patient Support Program info when I dropped this off, but I realized the $433 I paid was the full retail price. Could you please re-run this with the Lilly discount card?"

Tip: When you go back, ensure they have the card on file for your next refill so you don't have to troubleshoot this again. Since you are in Fort Saskatchewan, the Sherwood Park or Edmonton North Costcos are usually very experienced with these specific "re-billing" requests.
