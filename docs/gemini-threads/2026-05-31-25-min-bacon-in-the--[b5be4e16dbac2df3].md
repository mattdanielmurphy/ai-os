## Gemini

For a 25-minute cook time using convection, you should set your oven to 375°F (190°C).


Convection circulates hot air, which speeds up the rendering of fat and can cause the edges of the bacon to burn if the temperature is too high (like the standard 400°F). A slightly lower temperature ensures the fat renders fully and the meat crisps evenly without charring.


The Method for Perfection

* Start Cold: Place the bacon in the oven as you turn it on. Starting in a cold oven allows the fat to render slowly, which results in a flatter, crispier strip.

* Use a Rack: Place a wire cooling rack inside a rimmed baking sheet. Elevating the bacon allows the convection air to circulate 360 degrees around the meat, ensuring the bottom is as crispy as the top.

* Check Early: Because convection efficiency varies, start checking the bacon at the 20-minute mark. Thin-cut bacon may finish sooner, while thick-cut will likely need the full 25 minutes.

* The Carry-over Cook: Remove the bacon when it looks just slightly under your preferred doneness. It will continue to crisp up for 1–2 minutes as it sits on the paper towel.
