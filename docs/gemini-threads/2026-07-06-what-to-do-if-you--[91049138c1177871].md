## Gemini

Based on image.png, those "plastic circle things" are cam lock covers (or cam lock caps). They are purely cosmetic plugs used to hide the metal cam lock nuts common in flat-pack furniture.


If you've lost one, here are the easiest ways to handle it:


1. Get Free Replacements from the Manufacturer
If the furniture is from a major brand like IKEA, you can usually get replacements completely free:



* In-Store: Walk up to the customer service/returns desk; they often have spare parts bins filled with these.

* Online: Check the assembly manual for the specific 6-digit part number and order it directly from their website's spare parts page.


2. Buy Cheap Generics Online
If you don't know the brand, you can find multipacks online (Amazon, eBay, AliExpress) by searching for "cam lock cover caps" or "furniture hole plugs plastic."



* Just measure the diameter of the hole or one of your remaining caps (they are typically 12mm or 15mm) to ensure a snug snap-fit.


3. Quick DIY Alternatives
Because these pieces are non-structural, you can easily camouflage the open metal lock:



* Screw Cover Stickers: You can buy adhesive PVC screw hole covers that match the texture and color of your furniture. They lay completely flat and are barely noticeable.

* The Tape Trick: Cut a small circle out of matching gray or white electrical tape or vinyl wrap and place it over the hole.
