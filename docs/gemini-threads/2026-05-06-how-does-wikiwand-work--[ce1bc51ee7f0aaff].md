## Gemini

Wikiwand is a third-party interface designed to provide a modernized, visually enhanced experience for browsing Wikipedia articles. It is not owned or operated by the Wikimedia Foundation.


How it Functions
Wikiwand essentially acts as a frontend wrapper that takes the underlying content from Wikipedia and presents it through its own proprietary interface.



* Browser Extension: When you install the Wikiwand browser extension, it works by automatically intercepting requests to Wikipedia pages and redirecting your browser to the corresponding article on the Wikiwand website.

* Direct Access: You can also visit Wikiwand directly and search for topics there, rather than going to Wikipedia first.

* Data Source: The actual encyclopedia content remains sourced from Wikipedia; Wikiwand simply changes how that content is rendered and navigated.


Key Features
Wikiwand focuses on improving usability and aesthetics with several additions:



* Enhanced Navigation: A fixed sidebar that keeps the table of contents accessible while scrolling, link previews when hovering over internal links, and faster navigation.

* Modernized Layout: Updated typography, immersive cover images, galleries for media, and various customization options (such as different fonts and themes).

* AI Integration: Newer versions include features like AI-powered summaries, text-to-speech, and Q&A capabilities.


Important Considerations

* Privacy: Because Wikiwand is a commercial, ad-supported service, it operates differently from Wikipedia. It has been noted for its use of tracking cookies and data collection practices to support its business model, which is a point of consideration for privacy-conscious users.

* Independence: It is a separate entity. If you ever prefer to view the original, standard Wikipedia interface, Wikiwand typically provides an option or icon (often found within the article interface) to switch back to the original Wikipedia view.
