In physics and mathematics, variables are italicized to distinguish them from units, functions, or operators. Because $F_{fr}$ and $F_g$ are representing physical quantities (force), the entire symbol is treated as a variable. Therefore, it is standard practice to italicize the whole term: $F_{fr}$ and $F_g$.


This ensures consistency throughout your report, as it matches the formatting of other variables like $m$ (mass), $a$ (acceleration), and $v$ (velocity), which are also italicized.
