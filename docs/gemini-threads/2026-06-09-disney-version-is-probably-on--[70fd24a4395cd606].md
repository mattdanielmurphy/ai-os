## Gemini

Yes, the Disney+ version will look significantly better than the one on this page.


The forum post you are looking at is for a 720p BDRip from 2015, which was encoded from the old 2008 standard Blu-ray. That original 2008 master was highly criticized for being noisy, having poor black levels, and a somewhat soft, muddy texture.


By comparison, the version streaming on Disney+ has several major advantages:



* Native 4K Master: Disney released a brand-new, comprehensive 4K remaster of The Sixth Sense for its 25th anniversary. The streaming version utilizes this updated, high-resolution master.

* Resolution and Detail: It jumps from the forum's 720p resolution up to 4K (2160p). Reviews of the new transfer note a massive upgrade in clarity, allowing you to clearly read background text (like Dr. Crowe's handwritten patient notes) that is blurry on older versions.

* Better Color and Texture: The 4K transfer properly preserves the natural film grain and the moody, autumnal color palette of the film without the artificial digital noise or waxy skin tones present on the 2008 source file.


While a physical 4K disc always wins out on maximum bitrate and lossless audio, the 4K stream available on Disney+ easily outclasses this decade-old 720p encode.
