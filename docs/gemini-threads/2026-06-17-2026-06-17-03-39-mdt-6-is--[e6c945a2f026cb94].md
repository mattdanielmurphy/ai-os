## Gemini

They are not identical in mass, though they are very close.


A neutron is slightly heavier than a proton.



* Neutron mass: $\approx 1.6749 \times 10^{-27}$ kg

* Proton mass: $\approx 1.6726 \times 10^{-27}$ kg


The difference is small—roughly 0.14%—but it is physically significant. Because a free neutron is heavier, it is unstable and will eventually decay into a proton, an electron, and an antineutrino, with a mean lifetime of about 15 minutes. Within an atomic nucleus, however, the strong nuclear force and the configuration of the nucleons can render the neutron stable.
