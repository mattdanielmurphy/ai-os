Is there anyway to get my application feedback that I paid to apply to university of Alberta?

---

## Gemini

Yes, you can absolutely check your status and see any official feedback on your application, though what that feedback looks like depends on where your application currently stands.


Because you’ve already paid the application fee, the University of Alberta processes everything through their central online portal rather than sending paper mail.


Here is exactly how to find your feedback and track your application:


1. Access Your UAlberta Launchpad Portal
Within 48 hours of submitting and paying for your application, you should receive an email with your Campus Computing ID (CCID) and a temporary password.



* Log In: Go to the UAlberta Launchpad Portal using your account email.

* What to look for: This portal is your primary dashboard. It displays your 7-digit Student ID in the top-left corner and tracks your application's live progress.


2. Check the "Application Checklist" and "To-Do List"
Before an admissions officer can give a final decision, they review your files.



* If they need something else from you, they will leave advisor comments right here.

* This section will explicitly list if they are "Awaiting" specific transcripts, outstanding Grade 12 marks, or other missing documents.


3. Look for "Status Update" Letters
Once the admissions team evaluates your application, any official feedback or decisions will be posted under a Status Update heading on your Launchpad homepage. Whenever a new letter is posted, you will get an automated email alert telling you to log in and check it.



* If you are admitted: Your status letter will outline your official offer and any specific conditions you need to maintain (like keeping up your academic average or submitting final transcripts by the summer deadline).

* If the application is unsuccessful: The university will upload an official Feedback Letter directly to your Launchpad account. This letter outlines the decision based on the competitive admission averages or requirements for that specific intake year.




Note on processing times: Most standard high school applications are assessed within one to two weeks once all required marks are submitted, though transfer or specialized program applications can take longer.



If your portal is entirely blank, or if it has been more than a few days and you haven't received your CCID login info to access Launchpad, you can reach out directly to the Student Service Centre via their online portal or by phone at +1-780-492-8000 to have them verify your payment and account status.

## User

Is there anyway to get my application fee back that I paid to apply to university of Alberta?

---

## Gemini

Unfortunately, no. The University of Alberta strictly enforces a non-refundable policy for all undergraduate and graduate application fees ($100 to $150 depending on your student status).


The university charges this fee to cover the administrative cost of processing and evaluating documentation, so they do not issue refunds or grant retroactive waivers once the payment has gone through, regardless of whether you withdraw the application, change your mind, or are denied admission.


The only rare exceptions where you might see funds returned are if you experienced a technical glitch during checkout that resulted in a duplicate payment (i.e., you were accidentally charged twice for the exact same application), or if you accidentally paid a separate tuition/program deposit that you were actually exempt from. If you think you were double-charged due to a payment processing error, your best bet is to open a ticket immediately through the Student Service Portal to have them review the transaction ledger.
