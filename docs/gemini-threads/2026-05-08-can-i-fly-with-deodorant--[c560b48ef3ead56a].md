Can I fly with deodorant stick?

## Gemini

Yes, you can absolutely fly with a solid stick of deodorant in either your carry-on or checked luggage.


Since it is a solid, it is not subject to the TSA (or CATSA, if you're flying from Edmonton/Alberta) "3-1-1" liquids rule. You can bring a full-sized stick without having to put it in a clear quart-sized bag.


Important Distinctions:

* Gel or Spray Deodorant: These are considered liquids/gels. If you’re bringing these in a carry-on, they must be 3.4 ounces (100ml) or smaller and fit inside your clear liquids bag.

* Large Sprays: If you have a full-sized aerosol or large gel, those must go in your checked bag.


Pro-tip: If you're packing a solid stick in your carry-on, keep it near the top of your bag just in case security wants to give it a closer look, though they rarely ask to see solid sticks.
