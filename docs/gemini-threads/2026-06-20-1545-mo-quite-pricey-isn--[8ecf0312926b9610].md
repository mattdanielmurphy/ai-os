## Gemini

It definitely looks steep on the surface, but the math behind it works out a bit differently than a standard monthly rental agreement.


Because university housing operates on a compressed academic schedule rather than a full calendar year, the actual monthly cost breaks down like this:



* Total Contract Cost: $12,364

* Contract Length: 8 months (specifically from August 26 through April)

* Actual Monthly Cost: $1,545.50 per month


What's Actually Included
When comparing this to a standard apartment in the Edmonton area, it helps to factor in the utilities and extras that are bundled into that total:



* All Utilities: Heat, water, and power are fully covered.

* Connectivity: High-speed wireless internet is included.

* Furnishings: The room comes fully furnished (twin XL bed, desk, chair, mini-fridge, and storage).

* Community Perks: You're paying for proximity to classes (no transit costs or winter commutes) and access to the Global Education/Residence Life programming.


It is absolutely on the higher end for a single room, but when you strip out separate bills for internet, electricity, and furniture, it bridges some of the gap compared to living off-campus.
