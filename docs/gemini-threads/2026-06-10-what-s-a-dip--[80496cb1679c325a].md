any way to reduce canoeing for a preroll?

---

## Gemini

Yes, there are several reliable ways to prevent or fix "canoeing" (when one side of the joint burns down faster than the other). Canoeing is usually caused by uneven airflow, uneven packing, or an irregular light.


Here is how you can prevent and correct it:


1. The Right Way to Light It

* Don't inhale while lighting: Direct inhaling while applying a flame pulls the fire down one path of least resistance, causing an immediate uneven burn.

* Toast the tip: Hold the preroll at a 45-degree angle just above the flame (not directly in it). Rotate it slowly between your fingers to create an even, glowing cherry all the way around before you take your first puff.


2. Fix the Packing (The Roll/Massage)
Prerolls from dispensaries are notoriously packed unevenly—often too tight near the filter and too loose near the tip, or with air pockets down one side.



* The Massage: Before lighting, gently roll the preroll between your thumb and index finger starting from the filter up to the tip. This redistributes any clumped material and closes up internal air pockets.

* The Pack: If it feels incredibly loose, give it a few light taps, filter-side down, on a hard surface to settle the material evenly.


3. Smoking Technique

* Rotate as you puff: Make it a habit to rotate the joint a quarter-turn between every hit or two. This prevents gravity and minor drafts from favoring one side.

* Take smaller, gentler pulls: Hard, aggressive drags create intense heat that will rapidly exploit any minor air pocket or loose side, instantly causing a canoe.


4. How to Fix It Mid-Smoke
If it starts to canoe anyway, you can catch it early:



* The Saliva Trick: Apply a tiny amount of moisture with your finger directly below the running side (on the unburnt paper just underneath the fast-burning edge). This slows down that side, allowing the rest of the cherry to catch up.

* Burn the Excess: Use your lighter to carefully burn away the unburnt "canoe canoe flap" of paper so the cherry can reset evenly.
