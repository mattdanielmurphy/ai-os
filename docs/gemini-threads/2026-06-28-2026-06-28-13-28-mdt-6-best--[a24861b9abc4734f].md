## Gemini

Toronto offers a vibrant mix of distinct neighborhoods, cultural institutions, and striking viewpoints. If you are exploring the city for the first time, these are some of the most notable locations and experiences to add to your itinerary.


CN Tower
The CN Tower is the city's most recognizable landmark, defining the skyline at over 553 meters tall. Visitors head here for panoramic views of the downtown core and Lake Ontario, making it a great first stop to get a layout of the city.



* The View: Features floor-to-ceiling window walls and a glass floor section looking straight down to the street level.

* Thrill Seeking: Home to the EdgeWalk, a hands-free, outdoor walk around the outer ledge of the tower's main pod.

* Dining: Features a revolving 360 Restaurant that completes a rotation every 72 minutes and focuses on Canadian ingredients.


Distillery District
The Distillery District is a pedestrian-only historic neighborhood showcasing well-preserved Victorian industrial architecture. The cobblestone streets are highly picturesque and offer a relaxed atmosphere away from the modern skyscrapers.



* Art & Shopping: Lined with contemporary art galleries, indie boutiques, and artisan studios.

* Food & Drink: Known for local coffee shops, craft breweries, and diverse dining spots.

* Seasonal Events: Hosts massive public installations and outdoor markets throughout the year.


St. Lawrence Market
St. Lawrence Market is a historic culinary hub that has operated in some form since 1803. Housed in a massive building that once served as city hall, it provides an energetic environment filled with local vendors and exceptional fresh food.



* Diverse Vendors: Features more than 100 butchers, bakers, fishmongers, and artisans.

* Signature Eats: Highly recommended for trying local specialties like peameal bacon sandwiches.

* Weekend Markets: Expands on Saturdays with a dedicated farmers' market and Sundays with antique dealers.


Royal Ontario Museum
The Royal Ontario Museum is Canada's largest museum, combining historic architecture with a striking modern crystal expansion. It serves as an impressive destination for anyone interested in exploring human history and the natural world.



* Massive Collection: Houses millions of items across dozens of galleries covering world cultures and natural history.

* Key Exhibits: Notable for its extensive dinosaur fossil skeletons and Indigenous art collections.

* Guided Tours: Offers regular walks and expert-led gallery presentations included with general admission.


Art Gallery of Ontario
The Art Gallery of Ontario stands out as one of the premier art institutions in North America. The building itself is an architectural standout, featuring a stunning wood-and-glass facade designed by architect Frank Gehry.



* Expansive Artworks: Holds a collection of over 95,000 works spanning European masterpieces to contemporary art.

* Canadian Heritage: Features an unmatched collection of historic Canadian and Indigenous pieces, including the Group of Seven.

* Curated Shopping: The on-site AGO Shop is a popular stop for unique design goods and books.


Kensington Market
Kensington Market is a bohemian neighborhood celebrated for its multicultural roots and eclectic street life. It offers a distinct contrast to corporate downtown, filled with colorful Victorian homes repurposed into businesses.



* Global Dining: Packed with affordable, diverse eateries, specialty grocers, and cheese shops.

* Vintage Finds: A major destination for secondhand clothing stores, record shops, and quirky boutiques.

* Pedestrian Sundays: Car-free days during the warmer months turn the streets into a massive open-air festival.


Toronto Islands
The Toronto Islands provide a peaceful park escape just a short ferry ride away from the central waterfront. It is an ideal spot for outdoor recreation and offers some of the best unobstructed views of the city skyline.



* Activities: Features walking trails, bicycle rentals, swimming beaches, and a vintage amusement park.

* Transportation: Accessible via a regular public ferry system operating from the foot of Bay Street.

* Atmosphere: Completely car-free, making it highly relaxing for a half-day or full-day outing.


Are you more interested in focusing on historic neighborhoods and food, or would you prefer prioritizing museums and skyline views?

// Trigger the organization when you click the extension icon
chrome.action.onClicked.addListener(async () => {
  // 1. Fetch all normal tabs in the current window
  const tabs = await chrome.tabs.query({ currentWindow: true, pinned: false });
  
  // 2. Group tab IDs by their base domain
  const groupsByDomain = {};
  
  for (const tab of tabs) {
    if (!tab.url || tab.url.startsWith('chrome://')) continue;
    
    try {
      const urlObj = new URL(tab.url);
      // Extracts "example.com" from "sub.example.com" or "example.com"
      const domainParts = urlObj.hostname.split('.');
      const baseDomain = domainParts.slice(-2).join('.');
      
      if (!groupsByDomain[baseDomain]) {
        groupsByDomain[baseDomain] = [];
      }
      groupsByDomain[baseDomain].push(tab.id);
    } catch (e) {
      // Ignore invalid or unparseable URLs
    }
  }
  
  // 3. Create native Chrome Tab Groups for domains with multiple tabs
  for (const [domain, tabIds] of Object.entries(groupsByDomain)) {
    if (tabIds.length > 1) { // Only group if there are 2 or more tabs for a domain
      const groupId = await chrome.tabs.group({ tabIds });
      
      // Customize the native group visual properties
      await chrome.tabGroups.update(groupId, {

      });
    }
  }
});
