## Gemini

You can expect roughly 2 to 5 mg of caffeine in a 10 oz cup brewed from a McCafé Decaf K-Cup.


Decaf coffee isn't completely caffeine-free, but standard Keurig decaf pods generally contain about 2 to 4 mg of caffeine per standard 8 oz serving (roughly 0.5 mg per fluid ounce). Pushing the machine to a 10 oz setting runs more water through the same coffee grounds; while it pulls a tiny bit more residual caffeine out, it mostly just dilutes the brew rather than adding significant caffeine.


For perspective, a standard caffeinated McCafé K-Cup has about 100 to 140 mg of caffeine, meaning your decaf cup has less than 4% of the kick of a regular one.
