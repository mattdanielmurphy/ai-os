## Gemini

The "What the fuck?" is an entirely appropriate reaction, but there is some wild context behind that number. That isn't just a regular 2005 Dodge Ram; it’s the Ram SRT-10, which basically means Dodge’s engineers got bored and decided to shove a Viper engine into a pickup truck.


Here is why that specific one is being appraised at $74,000:


1. The "Viper" Factor
The "Viper Power Red Motor" mentioned in the text is an 8.3L V10. It produces 500 horsepower and 525 lb-ft of torque. In 2004, this thing actually set the Guinness World Record for the world's fastest production pickup (hitting nearly 250 km/h).


2. The Low Mileage
The image shows it has 41,754 km. For a 21-year-old vehicle, that is incredibly low (averaging only about 2,000 km a year). Collectors go crazy for these trucks when they have low mileage because most of them were driven hard, crashed, or modified into oblivion.


3. Market Rarity

* Production: They only made about 10,000 of these over a three-year run (2004–2006).

* Current Values: While a high-mileage SRT-10 might go for $30k–$40k, "pristine" examples have been skyrocketing. Some ultra-low-mileage versions (under 5,000 km) have recently sold at auctions like Bring a Trailer for over $100,000.
