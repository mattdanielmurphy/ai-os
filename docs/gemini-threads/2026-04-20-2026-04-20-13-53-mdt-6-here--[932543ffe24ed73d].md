The Residence Deposit: Is it refundable?
Generally, university residence deposits are non-refundable once paid, as they act as a commitment to the room. However, most institutions (including U of A and MacEwan) do have a "Safety Valve" for admission denials.



* MacEwan University: Their policy typically states that if you are denied admission to the university, you are eligible for a refund of your residence deposit (minus a small administrative fee, usually around $25–$50). If you are accepted but simply choose not to go, you lose the deposit.

* University of Calgary: Similar to MacEwan, the "Admission Deposit" (for the program) is non-refundable, but for Residence, if you can provide proof that you were not admitted to the university, they generally refund the deposit.

* The Catch for You: Because your grades are so high (90s), you are almost guaranteed admission to MacEwan. This means if you pay the $500 and then get into U of A, you likely won't get that $500 back from MacEwan, because you weren't "denied"—you just chose the other school.
