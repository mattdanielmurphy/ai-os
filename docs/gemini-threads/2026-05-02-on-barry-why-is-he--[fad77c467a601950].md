## Gemini

The name is a bit of geographic shorthand rather than a plot-driven mystery.


NoHo stands for North Hollywood, the neighborhood in Los Angeles where Hank’s branch of the Chechen mob operates.


It is essentially a territorial nickname. In the world of the show, it distinguishes him as the "North Hollywood" guy, similar to how crime figures or street artists might adopt the name of their home turf or the area they control.


Since you’re in Season 3, you’ve already seen plenty of him—rest assured, the name isn't a setup for a massive late-series twist; it’s just where he sets up shop.
