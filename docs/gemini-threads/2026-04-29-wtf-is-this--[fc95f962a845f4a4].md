## Gemini

This is a serving board (often called a paddle board or small charcuterie board), but specifically, it is a commemorative piece from the Kemano T2 Project.


The shape and size suggest it's intended for serving small items like cheese or appetizers, though its primary purpose here is as a souvenir for people who worked on or were involved with that specific engineering project.


What is the Kemano T2 Project?
The Kemano T2 (Tunnel 2) Project was a massive hydro-engineering undertaking by Rio Tinto in British Columbia. It involved:



* A Second Tunnel: Boring a second 16km tunnel through Mt. DuBose to ensure long-term power reliability for the BC Works aluminum smelter in Kitimat.

* Completion: The project had a long, complex history, but it was finally completed around mid-2022—which lines up exactly with the "Canada Day, 2022" engraving on your board.


Care Tip
If you plan on actually using it for food rather than just displaying it:



* Hand wash only: Never put it in the dishwasher, as it will warp and crack the wood.

* Oil it: Use food-grade mineral oil occasionally to keep the wood from drying out.
