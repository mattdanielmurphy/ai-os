This is the Rate Your Music (RYM) profile for a well-known and highly idiosyncratic user who goes by the username No_.


He is a prominent figure within the RYM community, largely famous for his massive volume of ratings and a highly specific, unconventional rating system.


Key Details About His Profile

* Community Status: He has been a member since August 2005 and has accumulated over 47,000 music ratings. He is widely discussed on the forums and frequently appears on community-made lists celebrating unique or famous RYM personalities.

* The "Inverse" Rating Curve: Unlike most users who rate the majority of their music 3 to 4 stars, No_ has rated a staggering 75% of his listened music (over 33,000 releases) as a 2.5 ("average to bad"). Conversely, he has handed out only 12 perfect 5.0 ratings.

He famously notes that he values novelty and music that successfully "zags" his expectations.

* Current Status: Based on comments from other users on his profile, he has been largely inactive or missing from the site for a while, leading to a lot of nostalgic and appreciative comments from the community asking for his return.
