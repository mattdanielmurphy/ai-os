## Gemini

If you are looking for a major format change or a brand-new project, there hasn't been a massive structural shift. Redbar is still operating in its usual rhythm.


Mike David and Jules are currently running Season 22, following their standard operational playbook:



* The Schedule: True to form, the release schedule remains completely unpredictable. Shows routinely drop with weeks of silence in between, usually followed by massive, marathon episodes (often pushing 6 to 8 hours long) to make up for the gaps.

* Recent Targets: The show continues its hyper-detailed, frame-by-frame breakdowns of the comedian podcast universe. Recent deep dives and commentary have focused on familiar circles like Joe DeRosa, Sam Tallent, Whitney Cummings, and the surrounding Austin/Roganesque comedy scenes, alongside occasional pivots into broader pop culture.

* Access: The full, unedited multi-hour episodes remain locked behind the Scars Club paywall on the official site, while shorter clips and segment previews trickle out onto public feeds or user-run archive channels.


If you are waiting on a specific episode drop, checking the Scars Club feed directly or monitoring the community boards is still the only real way to know when Mike decided to turn the cameras on.
