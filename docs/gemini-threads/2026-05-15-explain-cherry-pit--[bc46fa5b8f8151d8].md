## Gemini

You nailed it, but with a slight twist based on the board layout.


The clue "Bad thing to bite down on in a date" is a clever wordplay. It's referring to the fruit (a date), not a romantic outing.


While "cherry pit" fits the concept of biting a hard seed, the answer for this specific puzzle is PIT.


Looking at the grid, the selected word is 24-Across, which starts with T-O-E and has one empty square remaining before the black block. That gives it a total of 4 letters.


Because "PIT" is 3 letters, it is actually the answer for 22-Down:



* 22-Across is INN (3 letters).

* The square below the I in INN starts 24-Across (TOE...).

* This means 22-Down runs vertically through the I in INN and the T in TOE, perfectly spelling out P-I-T.
