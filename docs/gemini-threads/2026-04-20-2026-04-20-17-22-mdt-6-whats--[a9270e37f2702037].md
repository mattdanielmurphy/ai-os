Critical Caveats for 2026

* The "Humanizer" Loophole: If text has been run through a "humanizing" tool (like GPTHuman or StealthWriter), detection rates often drop below 50%.

* False Positives: Detectors frequently flag academic or technical writing because its structured, clear nature mimics AI patterns. This is especially prevalent for ESL (English as a Second Language) writers.

* Institutional Use: If you are checking for university purposes, Turnitin is the heavyweight, but it's only available through institutional licenses. It is often less sensitive than Winston or Originality but more integrated into the grading workflow.


Pro-Tip for Accuracy
If you need a definitive "vibe check," don't rely on a single score. Compare the results from Winston AI (for precision) and GPTZero (for academic patterns). If both flag the content, the probability is high; if they disagree, the text likely sits in a "grey area" of heavy editing or highly structured human writing.
