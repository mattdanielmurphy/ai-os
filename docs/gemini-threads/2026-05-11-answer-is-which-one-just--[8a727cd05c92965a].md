$$9 \times 2.8 = 25.2$$

The answer is the 1st option ($\frac{3\pi}{4}$ radians).


Explanation
To convert degrees to radians, you multiply the degree measure by the conversion factor $\frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$:



* Set up the multiplication:
$$135^\circ \times \frac{\pi}{180^\circ}$$

* Simplify the fraction:
Both 135 and 180 are divisible by 45.

* $135 \div 45 = 3$

* $180 \div 45 = 4$




* Result:
$$\frac{3\pi}{4}$$
