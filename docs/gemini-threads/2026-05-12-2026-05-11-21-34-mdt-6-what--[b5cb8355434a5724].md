## Gemini

If your iPad isn't appearing as a Screen Mirroring or Sidecar option on your Mac, it's usually a handshake issue between macOS and iPadOS. Since you likely want to use it as a second display or for Apple Pencil input, check these specific technical toggles:


1. The Continuity Basics
For Sidecar to work, both devices must be signed into the same Apple Account with Two-Factor Authentication enabled.



* Handoff: Ensure Handoff is toggled ON on both devices.

* Mac: System Settings > General > AirDrop & Handoff.

* iPad: Settings > General > AirPlay & Handoff.




* Bluetooth & Wi-Fi: Both must be active on both devices. They don't necessarily need to be on the same Wi-Fi network for a wired connection, but for wireless, they must be.


2. Trust the Connection (Wired)
If you are connecting via USB-C to avoid latency:



* Connect the iPad to your Mac.

* Open Finder on your Mac and select your iPad in the sidebar.

* If prompted, click Trust on the Mac and enter your passcode on the iPad to "Trust This Computer."

* If it still doesn't show, try a different port or cable. Sidecar can be finicky with low-quality third-party cables.


3. Firewall and AirPlay Settings
Sometimes the Mac’s security settings block the incoming signal from the iPad.



* AirPlay Receiver: Go to System Settings > General > AirPlay & Handoff. Ensure "AirPlay Receiver" is toggled ON. Set "Allow AirPlay for" to Current User.

* Firewall: Go to System Settings > Network > Firewall. If it's on, click Options and ensure "Block all incoming connections" is OFF.


4. Reset the Discovery Process
If the settings look correct but the iPad is still invisible:



* Toggle Bluetooth: Turn Bluetooth off and then back on for both the Mac and iPad simultaneously.

* Force Restart the iPad: Quickly press and release Volume Up, then Volume Down, then hold the Power button until the Apple logo appears.

* Kill the Sidecar Process: On your Mac, open Activity Monitor, search for Sidecar, and force quit any processes related to it. macOS will relaunch them automatically when you try to connect again.


5. Check Compatibility
Ensure "AirPlay and Handoff" is actually enabled within the iPad's settings under Settings > General. If you are using a VPN or a managed profile (Work/School) on either device, try disabling it, as these often block the local peer-to-peer discovery required for Sidecar.



Quick Verification:
Open System Settings > Displays on your Mac. Click the [+] icon (or "Add Display" dropdown). If the iPad is detected, it will appear here even if it's missing from the Control Center's Mirroring menu.
