# You can mix trufuel with normal fuel right

## Gemini

Yes, you can safely mix TruFuel with normal pump gas, but only if you match the engine type correctly (2-stroke vs. 4-stroke).


TruFuel is just high-quality, high-octane (typically 92+) ethanol-free gasoline. When you mix it with regular pump gas, you won't cause any mechanical damage, but you will dilute the benefits of the TruFuel.


Here is what you need to keep in mind based on what you are filling:


4-Stroke Engines (Mowers, pressure washers, etc.)

* Can you mix it? Yes.

* The result: Mixing TruFuel 4-Cycle with regular unleaded pump gas is completely fine. It just lowers the overall octane rating and introduces ethanol back into the system, depending on how much pump gas you add.


2-Stroke Engines (Trimmers, chainsaws, leaf blowers)

* Can you mix it? Yes, but both must be pre-mixed with oil.

* The rule: You can mix TruFuel 2-Cycle (either 40:1 or 50:1) with pump gas only if that pump gas has already been properly mixed with 2-cycle oil at the correct ratio.

* 

Warning: Never mix straight pump gas with TruFuel 2-Cycle into a 2-stroke engine, as you will lean out the oil ratio and risk seizing the engine.






Why do it?
Most people do this when they have half a tank of regular gas left but want to run TruFuel through the system before winter storage. Running a mix is fine, but for winterization, it's best to run the equipment until the regular gas is gone, then fill it entirely with TruFuel so the ethanol-free stabilizer can protect the carburetor fuel lines.
