## Gemini

If you're moving through Pre-Calculus 12 and need to go beyond the Khan Academy set, there are several high-quality resources that offer more technical depth and the interactive feedback you’re looking for.


Top Interactive Resources

* CEMC Courseware (University of Waterloo): This is one of the best free Canadian resources specifically aligned with the Pre-Calc 12 curriculum.

* How it works: Under the "Exponential and Logarithmic Functions" unit, you can find "Check Your Understanding" sections. These are interactive forms where you enter your answer, and it gives immediate feedback. If you get it wrong, you can often generate a new version of the same problem to try again.

* Focus: Laws of logarithms, solving logarithmic equations, and change of base.




* DeltaMath: Extremely popular in high school math for its "endless" practice feel.

* How it works: You can create a free student account. It is highly interactive, provides immediate correction, and—crucially—includes detailed step-by-step breakdowns of how to reach the correct answer.

* Focus: It has specific modules for "Expanding/Condensing Logs" and "Logarithmic Equations (Different Bases)."




* OpenStax Precalculus: If you want a more traditional textbook feel but with digital perks.

* How it works: Their online version of the textbook has practice problems at the end of every section (Chapter 4 covers logs). Most of these have "Show Answer" buttons for immediate verification.

Quick Knowledge Check
Since you're prepping for the final stretch of your course, keep an eye on the Change of Base Formula, as it’s a frequent hurdle in Pre-Calc 12:


$$\log_b(a) = \frac{\log_x(a)}{\log_x(b)}$$


This is particularly useful when you need to evaluate something like $\log_3(7)$ on a standard calculator by converting it to base 10 or base $e$.
