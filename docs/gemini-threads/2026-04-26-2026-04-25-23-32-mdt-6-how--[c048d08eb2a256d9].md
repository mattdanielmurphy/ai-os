## Gemini

It’s an old-school medical condition that used to be common back when people treated stomach ulcers by drinking huge amounts of milk and taking baking soda.


Essentially, it’s a "calcium overload." When you ingest too much calcium (the milk) alongside an absorbable alkali (the calcium carbonate in Tums), your blood chemistry can get knocked out of balance.


Why it’s relevant to you right now:

* The Triad: It causes a trio of issues: high blood calcium (hypercalcemia), overly alkaline blood (metabolic alkalosis), and stress on the kidneys.

* The Threshold: Most healthy people need to ingest a lot—usually over 4 grams of calcium—to trigger the acute version. To put that in perspective, a standard glass of milk has about 300mg, and a regular Tums has about 200-400mg.
