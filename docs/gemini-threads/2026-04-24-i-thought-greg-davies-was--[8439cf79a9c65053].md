## Gemini

The pronunciation of Greg's surname is actually "Davis" (rhyming with "pavis").


What you heard from Alex Horne in the early seasons was a long-running bit. Alex spent several of the first series intentionally mispronouncing Greg’s name as "Day-veez" to annoy him. It eventually became one of those early Taskmaster quirks that Alex phased out as the show's dynamic evolved, but "Davis" is the correct, intended pronunciation.


You'll notice in more recent series that Alex has (mostly) surrendered and stuck to the standard pronunciation.
