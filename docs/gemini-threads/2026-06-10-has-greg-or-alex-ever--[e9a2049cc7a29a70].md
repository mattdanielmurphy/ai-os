## Gemini

Yes, both of them have made appearances on Taskmaster: The Podcast, though one is a much more frequent visitor than the other.



* Alex Horne: "Little" Alex Horne was actually Ed Gamble's very first guest on Episode 1 of the podcast back in October 2020. He has returned multiple times since then, including a recurring segment called "Your BFF (Bonus Fact Finder)" where he shares behind-the-scenes trivia about the episodes.

* Greg Davies: The Taskmaster himself is a much rarer guest, but he did finally grace the studio for Episode 11 at the conclusion of Series 10. During his appearance, he and Ed debated his contentious point-scoring decisions and addressed some of the bizarre fan fiction surrounding his and Alex's on-screen chemistry.
