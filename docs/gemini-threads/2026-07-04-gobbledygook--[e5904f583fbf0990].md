## Gemini

Gobbledygook refers to language that is meaningless, repetitive, or impossible to understand due to excessive jargon, technical buzzwords, or garbled text.


The term was coined in 1944 by US Congressman Maury Maverick to describe the convoluted and confusing language used in official bureaucratic documents.
